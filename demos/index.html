Symbolic Demonstrations¶

Executable pure-symbolic Python demonstrations (Model(…, auto_intersect=False)) verifying every geometric construction and algebraic identity in Almagest Book I and Planisphaerium with zero floating-point coordinates.

1. Golden Pentagon–Decagon & Radical Chord Table¶

Constructs Ptolemy’s single-circle regular decagon ($s_{10} = R/\phi$) and regular pentagon ($s_5 = \sqrt{R^2 + s_{10}^2}$) over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$ (Almagest I.10a, Heiberg I, pp. 32–35), evaluates the closed-form SymPy radical tower for Ptolemy’s chord cascade ($120^\circ \to \dots \to \frac{3}{4}^\circ$), and verifies Ptolemy’s Inequality Lemma bounding $\operatorname{crd}(1^\circ) \approx 1;2,50$ and $\pi \approx 3;8,30 = \frac{377}{120}$.

 1r"""Demonstration 1: Ptolemy's Golden Pentagon-Decagon Construction & Radical Chord Table.
         2
         3Showcases *Almagest* Book I, Chs. 10-11 (Heiberg 1898, Vol. I.1, pp. 32-46):
         4
         51. Constructs Ptolemy's single-circle regular decagon ($DZ = R / \phi$) and regular
         6   pentagon ($BZ = \sqrt{R^2 + DZ^2}$) in ``geometor.model`` with ``auto_intersect=False``
         7   and zero floating-point coordinates.
         82. Computes the exact closed-form SymPy radical expressions and base-60 sexagesimal
         9   approximations for Ptolemy's chord cascade ($120^\circ \to 90^\circ \to 72^\circ \to
        10   60^\circ \to 36^\circ \to 12^\circ \to 6^\circ \to 3^\circ \to 1\frac{1}{2}^\circ
        11   \to \frac{3}{4}^\circ$), comparing each against Heiberg's Greek text.
        123. Verifies Ptolemy's Inequality Lemma bounds for $\operatorname{crd}(1^\circ) \approx 1;2,50$
        13   and the resulting sexagesimal ratio $\pi \approx 3;8,30 = \frac{377}{120}$.

2. Ptolemy’s Theorem on Cyclic Quadrilaterals & Menelaus Calculus¶

Constructs rational cyclic quadrilaterals (pythagorean_65 with integer sides $25, 39, 60, 52$ and integer diagonals $56, 63$ where $56 \times 63 = 3528 = 25 \times 60 + 39 \times 52$, plus brahmagupta with perpendicular diagonals) alongside Ptolemy’s Planar & 3D Spherical Menelaus lemmas and I.14 solar declinations.

 1r"""Demonstration 2: Ptolemy's Theorem on Cyclic Quadrilaterals & Menelaus Sector Lemma.
         2
         3Showcases *Almagest* Book I, Ch. 10b (Heiberg 1898, Vol. I.1, pp. 36-37) and Ch. 13-14
         4(pp. 68-78):
         5
         61. Constructs the Pythagorean cyclic quadrilateral on diameter $65$ with exact integer
         7   sides $(AB, BC, CD, DA) = (25, 39, 60, 52)$ and diagonals $(AC, BD) = (56, 63)$,
         8   verifying $56 \times 63 = 3528 = 25 \times 60 + 39 \times 52 = 1500 + 2028$ and
         9   locating Ptolemy's exact Euclidean similarity point $E \in AC$ ($AE = \frac{500}{21}$,
        10   $EC = \frac{676}{21}$).
        112. Constructs the pure-integer Brahmagupta cyclic quadrilateral on $x^2 + y^2 = 65^2$.
        123. Proves Ptolemy's Theorem universally over $\mathbb{Z}[t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4]$ and runs
        13   Ptolemy's Planar & 3D Spherical Menelaus verifications (*Almagest* I.13-I.14).
        14"""

3. Planisphaerium Stereographic Projection & Astrolabe Plate¶

Proves universal stereographic circle-preservation, the conformal metric pullback $ds^2 = \frac{4(dx^2 + dy^2)}{(1 + x^2 + y^2)^2}$, and §4 circular inversion ($r_+ \cdot r_- = R_{\text{eq}}^2$), and constructs the exact rational astrolabe plate ($R_{\text{eq}} = 60$, $r_{\text{Can}} = 40$, $r_{\text{Cap}} = 90$, oblique ecliptic center $(0, 25)$ and radius $65$).

  1r"""Demonstration 3: Stereographic Projection & Astrolabe Geometry (*Planisphaerium*).
          2
          3Showcases Ptolemy's *Planisphaerium* (ed. J. L. Heiberg, *Opera Astronomica Minora*,
          41907, §§1-4, pp. 227-234):
          5

          7   showing that every plane $aX + bY + cZ + d = 0$ ($d \neq c$) projects from the South
          8   Pole $S = (0, 0, -1)$ onto an exact Euclidean circle in the equatorial plane, while
          9   planes through $S$ ($d = c$, such as meridians) project to straight lines.
         102. Verifies the conformal metric tensor $J^T J = \frac{4}{(1 + x^2 + y^2)^2} I_2$ and
         11   *Planisphaerium* §4 circular inversion $r_+ \cdot r_- = R^2$ (`ez : ed = ed : ek`).
         123. Constructs Ptolemy's *Planisphaerium* §1 astrolabe plate (equator $ABGD$, Cancer $TL$,
         13   Capricorn $KM$, and oblique ecliptic $MBTD$) in ``geometor.model`` with zero ``sp.Float``
