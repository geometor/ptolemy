Section 17 · Non-Concentricity of Projected Parallels to the Zodiac¶

Planisphaerium, Section 17 (Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 254–255)¶

Circles parallel to the zodiac designated in this manner must always have different centers. For let there be as before the meridian circle abgd around center e, axis line bed, diameter of the equinoctial circle line ag, and diameters of circles parallel to the zodiac lines zh and tk; and let lines dlz, dmh, dnt, and dck be drawn. Next we describe circle dyf around triangle dnc, and having drawn line yf, we divide line lm into halves at point o. Since therefore it is certain that the circle whose diameter is zh can be described about diameter lm, and thus the circle whose diameter is tk about diameter nc, I say that these two circles are by no means of the same center—that is, point o is not at all the midpoint on diameter nc. For since arc zt equals arc kh, arc yn will also equal arc cf; whence lines lm and fy are parallel. Therefore, the ratio of line dl to line ly is the same as that of line dm to mf. Moreover,

the ratio of line dl to ly is the same as that of the square on dl to the rectangle contained by dl and ly, and the ratio of dm to line mf is the same as that of the square on dm to the rectangle contained by dm and mf. Since therefore, by reason of the circle, dl by ly equals lc by ln, and likewise dm by mf equals nm by cm, the ratio of the square on dl to cl by ln will be the same as that of the square on dm to mn by cm; alternately, therefore, the ratio of the square on dl to the square on dm is the same as that of the rectangle produced from cl and ln to the rectangle constituted from nm and cm. Now the square on dm is greater than the square on dl, just as dm is longer than dl; so therefore nm by cm is greater than cl by ln. Since therefore the common middle segment nc with cm in mc is greater than with nl in ln, it is clear that cm is greater than nl. Now mo is given equal to lo; hence it follows that oc is less than on. Therefore point o cannot be the midpoint on diameter nc; and since it is given as the midpoint on diameter ml, it is impossible for circles parallel to the zodiac to have the same center.
