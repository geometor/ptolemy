Section 12 · Notes¶

Manuscript Numerals & Almagest II.8 Verification¶

  1. Scribal Error (\(8^\circ 38'\) vs. VIIII puncta XXXVIII, \(9^\circ 38'\)): On p. 247, lines 11–20, Hermann’s Latin prints VIIII puncta XXXVIII (\(9^\circ 38'\)) as half of \(17^\circ 16'\). Yet \(\frac{1}{2}(17^\circ 16') = 8^\circ 38'\), and Ptolemy’s resulting ascensions—\(27^\circ 50' - 8^\circ 38' = 19^\circ 12'\) and \(27^\circ 50' + 8^\circ 38' = 36^\circ 28'\)—confirm that the original calculation used \(8^\circ 38'\).

  2. Line \(ey\) Variant & Almagest II.8 Agreement: Heiberg prints XII cum punctis XXII secundis XII on p. 247, line 5, noting in the critical apparatus that MS E reads 23 (\(12;23,12^p\), matching Section 8). In Almagest II.8 for Rhodes (\(14\frac{1}{2}^h\)), Aries and Pisces rise in \(19^\circ 12'\) and Virgo and Libra in \(36^\circ 28'\)—exact agreement.
