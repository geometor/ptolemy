Section 12 · Summary¶

Oblique Ascensions for Rhodes (\(\varphi = 36^\circ\)): First Sign¶

Applying the bisection method to the horizon passing through \(t\) (\(30^\circ\) from the equinox, radius \(et = 73;39,7^p\)) and centered at \(c\) (\(ec = 82;35,3^p\)):

  • Orthogonally projecting \(cy \perp tl\): \(ey = \frac{1}{2}(et - 3600/et) = 12;23,12^p\) (printed \(12;22,12^p\)).

  • Rescaled to \(ec = 120^p\): \(ey \approx 18^p = \operatorname{crd}(17^\circ 16')\).

  • Ascensional difference \(am = \angle tem = \angle ecy = \frac{1}{2}(17^\circ 16') = 8^\circ 38'\) (printed as VIIII puncta XXXVIII, i.e., \(9^\circ 38'\), in Hermann’s Latin).

  • Aries & Pisces: \(27^\circ 50' - 8^\circ 38' = 19^\circ 12'\).

  • Virgo & Libra: \(27^\circ 50' + 8^\circ 38' = 36^\circ 28'\).
