Section 16 · Notes¶

Stereographic Preservation of Small Circles¶

  1. Euclidean Circle Theorems Used: Ptolemy’s proof establishes circle preservation for an inclined small circle without invoking Apollonius’s Conics (subcontrary sections of an oblique cone). Instead, he applies Thales’ theorem (Eucl. III.31), the inscribed-angle theorem (Eucl. III.21–22), and the intersecting-chord power theorem (Eucl. III.35).

  2. Diagram & Apparatus Notes: The figure on Heiberg p. 253 depicts points dfamecngtkqhlzbyr, where the intersection of bz and kl is labeled q. In the apparatus on p. 253, concurrant ad punctum r carries minor manuscript variants for procedent and quod postremo.
