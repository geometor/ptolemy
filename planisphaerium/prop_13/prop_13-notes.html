Section 13 · Notes¶

Clima of Rhodes & Transmission Apparatus¶

  1. The Fourth Clima (Island of Rhodes): Ptolemy computes the oblique ascensions for the fourth clima (Rhodes, latitude \(36^\circ\) N, longest day \(14^h 30^m\)) as the central paradigm of the seven habitable climata, matching the rising-time tables in Almagest II.8.

  2. Textual & Diagram Witnesses: The Heiberg figure on p. 248 labels points ztacmybedhlgn. In the critical apparatus on p. 249, manuscript A preserves a gloss noting the Arabic term zernen / zemenen (zaman, time) behind Hermann’s rendering spatiis temporis.
