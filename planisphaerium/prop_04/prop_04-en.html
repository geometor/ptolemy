Section 4 · Circular Inversion & Astrolabe Radii from the Almagest¶

Planisphaerium, Section 4 (Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 232–235)¶

Theorem on Inverse Proportionality of Parallels (Heiberg II, pp. 232–233)¶

p. 232 These things having been established, we must now consider the ratio of the semidiameters of the parallel circles designated above the signs of the oblique circle to the semidiameter of the right circle, until we apprehend their rising and measure them with a definite number, as appears in the corporeal sphere.

p. 233 Let the equinoctial circle abgd be described around center e, whose diameters cutting each other orthogonally are ag and db. We extend ag straight to point z, and around g cut off two equal arcs gt and gh. Straight lines dkh and dtz are drawn, so that the northern parallel is made around center e at radius eh and the southern parallel around the same center at radius ez.

I say that as ez is to ed, so is ed to eh:

$$\frac{ez}{ed} = \frac{ed}{eh} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad ez \cdot eh = ed^2 = R_{\text{eq}}^2.$$

Demonstration. Since arc gh and arc gt are equal, arcs bt and bh together equal a semicircle ($180^\circ$); whence it follows that angles bdt and bdh together equal a right angle ($90^\circ$).

Moreover, in right triangle edh, angles edh and ehd together equal a right angle. Therefore the two right-angled triangles edh and edz are similar!

Whence it is necessary that as ez is to ed, so is ed to eh.

Numerical Radii Derived from the Almagest (Heiberg II, pp. 234–235)¶

p. 234 These things being held, we measure first each of arcs gh and gt as $23^\circ 51' 20''$ out of the $360^\circ$ measuring the right circle, which is the distance of each tropic from the equinoctial on the solid sphere.

According to this quantity of distance:

  • Arc bt $= 90^\circ + 23^\circ 51' 20'' = 113^\circ 51' 20''$.

  • Arc bh (residue to a semicircle) $= 180^\circ - 113^\circ 51' 20'' = 66^\circ 8' 40''$.

  • Chord of arc bt $= 100;33,28^p$ out of $120^p$ for the diameter, as was established in the Almagest (quemadmodum in Almagesti constitutum est).

  • Chord of arc bh $= 65;29,0^p$.

Therefore the ratio of $100;33,28^p$ to $65;29,0^p$ is the ratio of line ez to line ed, and of ed to line eh.

Since therefore ed, the semidiameter of the right circle, is set absolutely to $60^p$:

  1. Winter Tropic (Capricorn) Semidiameter:

    $$r_{\text{Cap}} = ez = 92;8,15^p \qquad (\text{Latin: }\textit{partibus XCII puncta VIII secunde XV}).$$

  2. Summer Tropic (Cancer) Semidiameter:

    $$r_{\text{Can}} = eh = 39;4,19^p \qquad (\text{Latin: }\textit{partes XXXIX puncta IIII secunde XVIIII}).$$

p. 235 3. Total Ecliptic Diameter & Center Offset:

  • Total Diameter ($ez + eh$): $131;12,34^p$.

  • Semidiameter of the Zodiac: $65;36,17^p$.

  • Distance of Zodiac Center from Equinoctial Center e:

    $$d_{\text{ecl}} = 65;36,17^p - 39;4,19^p = 26;31,58^p.$$
