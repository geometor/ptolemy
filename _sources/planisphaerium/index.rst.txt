===========

Table of Contents
-----------------

Composed at Alexandria by :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>` and translated into Latin in 1143 by :person:`Hermann of Carinthia <hermann>`, the *Planisphere* (*Planisphaerium*) is the foundational ancient text establishing the mathematical geometry of **stereographic projection** and the planispheric astrolabe. Each of the twenty sections of this **Multi-Track Book Codex** provides synchronized reading tracks (**English**, **Latin**, **Summary**, and **Notes**) wired to Heiberg's 1907 critical witness.

.. collection::
   :type: section
   :sort: order

For Ptolemy's primary mathematical treatise on spherical cosmology and chord trigonometry, see :doc:`The Almagest <../almagest_geometry/index>`.
