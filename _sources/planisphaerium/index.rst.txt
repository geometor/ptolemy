Composed at Alexandria by :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>` and translated into Latin in 1143 by :person:`Hermann of Carinthia <hermann>`, the *Planisphaerium* is the foundational ancient text establishing the mathematical geometry of **stereographic projection** and the planispheric astrolabe. Each section of this **Multi-Track Book Codex** provides synchronized reading tracks (**English**, **Latin (1907)**, **Summary**, and **Notes**) wired to interactive diagrams.
