Section 3 · Summary
===================

Geometric Principles of Section 3
---------------------------------

1. **Circle-Intersection Power of a Point**:
   - In circle $hatg$ (the horizon), chords $ag$ and $ht$ intersect at center $e$.
   - By Euclid III.35, $ae \cdot eg = he \cdot et$.
   - Since $a, b, g, d$ are on the equatorial circle around center $e$, the equatorial radii satisfy $ae = be = eg = ed = R_{\text{eq}}$.
   - Therefore $be \cdot ed = he \cdot et$.

2. **Concyclicity Criterion**:
   - By the converse of Euclid III.35, if $be \cdot ed = he \cdot et$, the four points $b, d, h, t$ lie on a single circle.
   - Since the zodiac circle was defined through $b, d$ and one of these intersection points, it necessarily contains the other intersection point $t$.

3. **Collinearity through the Center**:
   - The line joining the two horizon-zodiac intersection points $h$ and $t$ passes directly through the center $e$ of the equatorial circle.
   - This proves that the horizon cuts the ecliptic at diametrically opposite points ($180^\circ$ apart on the sphere).
