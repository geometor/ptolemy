Section 3 · Summary
===================

Geometric Principles of Section 3
---------------------------------

1. **Circle-Intersection Power of a Point**:
   - In circle :math:`hatg` (the horizon), chords :math:`ag` and :math:`ht` intersect at center :math:`e`.
   - By Euclid III.35, :math:`ae \cdot eg = he \cdot et`.
   - Since :math:`a, b, g, d` are on the equatorial circle around center :math:`e`, the equatorial radii satisfy :math:`ae = be = eg = ed = R_{\text{eq}}`.
   - Therefore :math:`be \cdot ed = he \cdot et`.

2. **Concyclicity Criterion**:
   - By the converse of Euclid III.35, if :math:`be \cdot ed = he \cdot et`, the four points :math:`b, d, h, t` lie on a single circle.
   - Since the zodiac circle was defined through :math:`b, d` and one of these intersection points, it necessarily contains the other intersection point :math:`t`.

3. **Collinearity through the Center**:
   - The line joining the two horizon-zodiac intersection points :math:`h` and :math:`t` passes directly through the center :math:`e` of the equatorial circle.
   - This proves that the horizon cuts the ecliptic at diametrically opposite points (:math:`180^\circ` apart on the sphere).
