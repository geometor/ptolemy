Section 6 · Summary
===================

Stereographic Radii at 60° from the Tropics
-------------------------------------------

Applying the inversion relation to the parallels at zodiacal longitude :math:`\lambda = 30^\circ` from the equinoxes (i.e., :math:`60^\circ` below the solstices), where :math:`\delta = 11^\circ 39' 59''`:

- Colure arcs: :math:`90^\circ + \delta = 101^\circ 39' 59''` and :math:`90^\circ - \delta = 78^\circ 20' 1''`.
- Chords (:math:`D = 120^p`): :math:`\operatorname{crd}(101^\circ 39' 59'') = 93;2,14^p` and :math:`\operatorname{crd}(78^\circ 20' 1'') = 75;47,23^p`.

- Southern parallel radius (Pisces/Capricorn–Scorpio side, :math:`ez`): :math:`73^p 39' 7''` (decimal :math:`73.652`).
- Northern parallel radius (Aries/Taurus–Virgo boundary, :math:`ek`): :math:`48^p 52' 42''` (decimal :math:`48.878`).
- Inversion product check: :math:`73;39,7 \times 48;52,42 \approx 3600 = 60^2`.
