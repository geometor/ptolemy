Section 6 · Parallel Circles at 60° from the Tropics
====================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 6 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 235–236 <heiberg:1907>`)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/fig09_planisphaerium_stereographic_astrolabe.svg

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0445
   :page: p. 235

No differently, if we set each of the arcs :g:`gh` and :g:`gt` to :math:`11^\circ 39' 59''`, which is the distance between the equinoctial circle and the parallels sixty degrees below the tropic points, the whole arc :g:`bt` will be :math:`101^\circ 39' 59''`, its chord :math:`93^p 2' 14''`, while arc :g:`bh` will be :math:`78^\circ 20' 1''`, and its chord :math:`75^p 47' 23''`.

Since, therefore, the ratio of :math:`93^p 2' 14''` to :math:`75^p 47' 23''` is the same as that of line :g:`ez` to line :g:`ed <BD_ast>`

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0446
   :page: p. 236

and of :g:`ed <BD_ast>` to line :g:`ek <EK>`, out of those parts of which :math:`60^p` fill line :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, line :g:`ez` must measure :math:`73^p 39' 7''`, and line :g:`ek <EK>` :math:`48^p 52' 42''`.
