Section 6 · Notes
=================

Zodiacal Parallels & Manuscript Numerals
----------------------------------------

1. **Declination at 30° from the Equinoxes**:
   The arc :math:`11^\circ 39' 59''` comes from Ptolemy's *Almagest* I.15 declination table at :math:`\lambda = 30^\circ` (the boundaries between Aries/Taurus, Virgo/Libra, Aquarius/Pisces, and Scorpio/Sagittarius). Its southern stereographic radius :math:`73;39,7^p` serves directly as a primary datum in Sections 8 and 12.

2. **Orthographic Consistency**:
   Heiberg's critical apparatus on p. 236 notes that MS A reads ``LXLVIII`` for ``XLVIII`` and ``XLII`` for ``LII``; Heiberg restores ``XLVIII puncta LII secundas XLII`` (:math:`48^p 52' 42''`), which satisfies the exact inverse proportion :math:`73;39,7 \cdot 48;52,42 = 3599;59,25 \approx 60^2`.
