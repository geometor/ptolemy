Section 9 · Rising of the Signs in the Right Sphere: Second and Third Signs
===========================================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 9 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 240–241 <heiberg:1907>`)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0450
   :page: p. 240

By a like example one may also measure the rising of the following signs: if we set arc :g:`bk` of the oblique circle to an arc of two signs, so that point :g:`k` marks the beginning of Aquarius and point :g:`l` the beginning of Sagittarius—whose diametrically opposite points are :g:`n`, the beginning of Leo, and :g:`y`, the beginning of Gemini—
when the rest are drawn in like manner, :g:`kt` and :g:`te` will remain of the same quantity, while line :g:`ke`

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0451
   :page: p. 241

will increase, as has been demonstrated, so that the semidiameter of the parallel circle designated at the beginning of Aquarius and Sagittarius measures :math:`86^p 29' 42''`.

If, therefore, the difference stated above—that is, :math:`3600^p`—be divided by this line, there will be gathered the excess of line :g:`kf` over line :g:`fe`, which is :math:`41^p 38' 18''`. When this has been subtracted from line :g:`ke`, there remain :math:`44^p 51' 24''`, half of which—that is, :math:`22^p 25' 42''`—must bound line :g:`fe`, out of those parts of which :math:`26^p 31' 58''` count line :g:`et`.
Therefore, out of those parts of which :math:`120^p` constitute line :g:`et`, opposite the right angle, line :g:`fe` will be :math:`101^p 28'`, and the arc of chord :g:`fe` will be :math:`115^\circ 28'` out of the :math:`360^\circ` of the whole circle containing right triangle :g:`fet`. Accordingly, out of the :math:`360^\circ` in four right angles, angle :g:`∠fte` will have :math:`57^\circ 44'`.
To this angle :g:`∠bek` is equal; and since it is at the center of the equinoctial circle, arc :g:`bm` must also be of that quantity. Whence, with the portion of Pisces subtracted, the portion of Aquarius remains :math:`29^\circ 54'`.
And from the necessity established above, it follows that this same quantity belongs to the remaining three signs standing at the same distance from the equinoctial points—namely Taurus, Leo, and Scorpio; whence it follows that what remains from the quadrant, that is, from :math:`90^\circ`, measures the quantity of the rising of the remaining four—namely Gemini and Cancer, Sagittarius and Capricorn.
