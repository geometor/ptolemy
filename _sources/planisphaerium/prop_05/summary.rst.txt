Section 5 · Summary
===================

Stereographic Radii at 30° from the Tropics
-------------------------------------------

Using the inversion law :math:`r(-\delta)/60 = 60/r(+\delta) = \operatorname{crd}(90^\circ+\delta)/\operatorname{crd}(90^\circ-\delta)` for the parallels at zodiacal longitude :math:`\lambda = 60^\circ` (declination :math:`\delta = 20^\circ 30' 9''`):

- Colure arcs: :math:`90^\circ + \delta = 110^\circ 30' 9''` and :math:`90^\circ - \delta = 69^\circ 29' 51''`.
- Chords (:math:`D = 120^p`): :math:`\operatorname{crd}(110^\circ 30' 9'') = 98;35,59^p` and :math:`\operatorname{crd}(69^\circ 29' 51'') = 68;23,51^p`.

- Southern parallel radius (Aquarius/Capricorn–Scorpio boundary, :math:`ez`): :math:`86^p 29' 42''` (decimal :math:`86.495`).
- Northern parallel radius (Taurus/Gemini–Leo boundary, :math:`ek`): :math:`41^p 39' 15''` (decimal :math:`41.654`).
- Equinoctial scale: :math:`R_{\text{eq}} = 60^p`.
