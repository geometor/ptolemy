Section 18 · Notes
==================

Maslama al-Majrīṭī's Scholia & Diagram Apparatus
------------------------------------------------

1. **Maslama's Note Excluded by Heiberg**:
   In the critical apparatus on Heiberg p. 257, the editor notes that he omitted from the diagram on p. 256 elements referring to a scholium by :person:`Maslama al-Majrīṭī <maslama>` (*In fig. p. 256 omisi, quae ad notam Maslemi referuntur*), whose Arabic commentary accompanied Hermann's 1143 Latin translation.

2. **Dual Figures & Practical Astrolabe Truncation**:
   Heiberg prints two figures for this section: the meridional projection on p. 256 (:g:`y, m, d, t, z, f, k, h, q, a, e, g, c, l, b`) and the planispheric plate on p. 257 (:g:`n, y, c, b, l, d, f, e, a, q`). Since :g:`o` falls off the physical plate, the arc is constructed directly via :g:`c` and :g:`y`.
