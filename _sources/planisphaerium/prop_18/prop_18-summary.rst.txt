Section 18 · Summary
====================

Boundary Construction for Invisible Southern Parallels
------------------------------------------------------

1. **Problem Statement**:
   A parallel to the zodiac with diameter :math:`tkl` may extend into the perpetually invisible southern region (inside the southern circle of perpetual invisibility :math:`zh`), falling outside the physical boundaries of the planispheric plate when projected from the south pole :math:`d`.

2. **Angular Reconstruction via Semicircle** :math:`zmh`:
   Projecting :math:`dhn`, :math:`dlc`, and :math:`dt` onto :math:`ag` gives :math:`c` and :math:`n` (with bounding parallel :math:`nyq`). Erecting :math:`km \perp zh` onto semicircle :math:`zmh` and setting :math:`\angle aey = \angle mfk` constructs :math:`y` on :math:`nyq` (with :math:`yq \sim mz`), defining the circle through :math:`c, y` and opposite point :math:`o`.
