Section 4 · Circular Inversion & Astrolabe Radii from the Almagest
==================================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 4 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 232–235 <heiberg:1907>`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/fig09_planisphaerium_stereographic_astrolabe.svg

Theorem on Inverse Proportionality of Parallels (`Heiberg II, pp. 232–233`)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0442
   :page: p. 232

These things having been established, we must now consider the ratio of the semidiameters of the parallel circles designated

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0443
   :page: p. 233

.. picture:: 0443.g1.png
   :alt: Folio 0443 diagram (archival scan)

.. picture:: 0443.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0443 diagram (interactive vector)

above the signs of the oblique circle to the semidiameter of the right circle, until we apprehend their rising and measure them with a definite number, as appears in the corporeal sphere—both upright and inclined.

Let the equinoctial circle :g:`abgd <cABGD>` be described around center :g:`e <E>`, whose diameters cutting each other orthogonally are :g:`ag <MK>` and :g:`db <BD_ast>`. We extend :g:`ag <MK>` straight to point :g:`z`, and around :g:`g <K>` cut off two equal arcs :g:`gt` and :g:`gh`. Straight lines :g:`dkh` and :g:`dtz` are drawn, in the manner we established, so that of the parallel circles the northern parallel is made around center :g:`e <E>` at radius :g:`ek <EK>`, and the southern parallel around the same center at radius :g:`ez`.

**I say that** as :g:`ez` is to :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, so is :g:`ed <BD_ast>` to :g:`ek <EK>`:

.. math::

   \frac{ez}{ed} = \frac{ed}{ek}.

*Demonstration.* Since arcs :g:`gh` and :g:`gt` are equal, arcs :g:`bt` and :g:`bh` together equal a semicircle; whence it follows that angles :g:`∠bdt` and :g:`∠bdk` together equal a right angle. Moreover, angles :g:`∠edk` and :g:`∠ekd` together equal a right angle; therefore the two right-angled triangles :g:`edk` and :g:`edz` are similar. Whence it is necessary that as :g:`ez` is to :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, so is :g:`ed <BD_ast>` to :g:`ek <EK>`.

Next we take up the ratios of the arcs and their chords as well. For it is manifest that the ratio of angle :g:`∠bdt` to angle :g:`∠ezd` is the same as that of arc :g:`bt` to arc :g:`td`, since it is equal to :g:`bh`—

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0444
   :page: p. 234

which is clearly also the ratio of arc :g:`ez` to arc :g:`ed <BD_ast>` on the circle designated about triangle :g:`edz`. Whence it follows that the ratio of lines :g:`ez` to :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, and of :g:`ed <BD_ast>` to :g:`ek <EK>`, is the same as the ratio of chord :g:`bt` to chord :g:`td`.

Numerical Radii Derived from the *Almagest* (`Heiberg II, pp. 234–235`)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

These things being held, we measure first each of arcs :g:`gh` and :g:`gt` as :math:`23^\circ 51' 20''` out of the :math:`360^\circ` measuring the right circle, which, as we stated earlier, is the equal distance of each tropic from the equinoctial on the corporeal sphere.

Accordingly, by this quantity of distance, arc :g:`bt` will be :math:`113^\circ 51' 20''` of the number that measures the whole circle as :math:`360^\circ` exactly, while arc :g:`bh`, the remainder of the semicircle, will be :math:`66^\circ 8' 40''`. And the straight line—that is, the chord of arc :g:`bt`—is :math:`100^p 33' 28''` out of those :math:`120^p` parts that measure the whole diameter of the circle, **as was established in the Almagest**, while the chord of :g:`bh` is :math:`65^p 29' 0''`.

Therefore the ratio of :math:`100^p 33' 28''` to :math:`65^p 29' 0''` is that of line :g:`ez` to line :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, and of :g:`ed <BD_ast>` to line :g:`ek <EK>`.

Since therefore :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, the semidiameter of the right circle, is exactly :math:`60^p` parts, :math:`92^p 8' 15''` of those parts measure line :g:`ez`, the semidiameter of the winter tropic, and :math:`39^p 4' 19''` measure the semidiameter of the summer tropic.

From this it follows—since these semidiameters joined together make the entire diameter of the zodiac, and taken together they are :math:`131^p 12' 34''`—that

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0445
   :page: p. 235

the semidiameter of the zodiac consists of :math:`65^p 36' 17''`, and its center stands distant from the equinoctial center by :math:`26^p 31' 58''`.
