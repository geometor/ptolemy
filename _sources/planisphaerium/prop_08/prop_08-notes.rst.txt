Section 8 · Notes
=================

Planar Proof of Right Ascension & Comparison with the *Almagest*
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. **Bisection Lemma (*Elements* II.5 / II.6 & Orthogonality)**:
   Ptolemy invokes the property that when the ecliptic circle (radius :math:`tk`) bisects the equinoctial circle (radius :math:`eb = 60^p`) along diameter :math:`bd`, triangle :math:`bet` is right-angled at :math:`e`, so :math:`tk^2 - te^2 = tb^2 - te^2 = eb^2 = 3600`.

2. **The "Easier Way" (*leviori modo*)**:
   Because points :math:`k` and :math:`n` are diametrically opposite on the sphere (though not collinear with :math:`t` on the plane), :math:`tk = tn`, so the altitude :math:`tf \perp kn` bisects :math:`kn` at :math:`f`. Combined with the inversion product :math:`ke \cdot en = be \cdot ed = 3600`, this immediately yields :math:`ke - en = 2fe`.

3. **Agreement with Spherical Trigonometry**:
   In *Almagest* I.16, Ptolemy computes :math:`\tan \alpha = \cos \varepsilon \tan \lambda` via Menelaus's Theorem on the sphere; for :math:`\lambda = 30^\circ` and :math:`\varepsilon = 23^\circ 51' 20''`, :math:`\alpha(30^\circ) = 27^\circ 50'` identically, demonstrating that the stereographic planisphere reproduces spherical astronomy purely in the plane.
