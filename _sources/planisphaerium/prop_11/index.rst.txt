Section 11 · Difference of Days in the Clime of Rhodes
======================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 11 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 244–245 <heiberg:1907>`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0454
   :page: p. 244

With these things also known, we must first see whether in this clime the difference of its days, which we have set forth, agrees with what occurs in the corporeal sphere. We shall therefore describe a figure of this kind, and in it, as before, the horizon through points :g:`z`, :g:`k`, :g:`h`, :g:`l` singly.

In order, therefore, to apprehend what we intend—namely the quantity of arc :g:`ak`—we shall fix as before the center of the horizon at point :g:`c` and draw lines :g:`ct` and :g:`ce` perpendicular to lines :g:`zh` and :g:`kl`. Since, therefore, as was established, :math:`82^p 35' 3''` measure line :g:`ce`, the distance of the centers of the equinoctial circle and of the horizon of this clime, out of those parts

of which :math:`26^p 31' 58''` contain line :g:`et`, the distance of the centers of the equinoctial and of the zodiac, out of those parts of which we count :math:`120^p` in line :g:`ec` opposite the right angle, it will be :math:`38^p 33'`, the arc of whose chord is :math:`37^\circ 30'` out of the :math:`360^\circ` of the whole circle containing triangle :g:`ect`.
Therefore, out of the :math:`360^\circ` that we count in four right angles, angle :g:`∠ect` will contain :math:`18^\circ 45'`, while angle :g:`∠cet`, completing a right angle with it, will contain :math:`71^\circ 15'`. Hence angle :g:`∠aek` must also consist of :math:`18^\circ 45'`, whence it follows that arc :g:`ak` is of the same quantity.
Accordingly, :math:`71^\circ 15'` measure the rising of each quadrant from the vernal equinox, and :math:`108^\circ 45'` from the autumnal equinox; whence the difference of the longest and shortest days from the equinoctial day is :math:`37^\circ 30'`, which are equal to two hours and a half, just as was established in the corporeal sphere.
