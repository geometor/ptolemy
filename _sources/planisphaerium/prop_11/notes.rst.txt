Section 11 · Notes
==================

Planar Calculation vs. Spherical Trigonometry (*Almagest* II.3 & II.6)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. **Direct Trigonometric Identity**:
   In stereographic projection, :math:`et = R \tan \varepsilon` and :math:`ec = R \cot \varphi`. Thus in right triangle :math:`ect`, :math:`\sin(\angle ect) = \frac{et}{ec} = \tan \varphi \tan \varepsilon`. Using :math:`\varphi = 36^\circ` and :math:`\varepsilon = 23^\circ 51' 20''`, :math:`\arcsin(\tan 36^\circ \tan 23^\circ 51' 20'') \approx 18^\circ 45'`, identical to Ptolemy's planar chord calculation.

2. **Rhodes Clime Calibration**:
   In *Almagest* II.6, the parallel of Rhodes is defined precisely as the latitude where the longest day is :math:`14\frac{1}{2}` hours (:math:`+2\frac{1}{2}` hours above the :math:`12^h` equinoctial day). Ptolemy confirms here that the stereographic planisphere reproduces this foundational clime parameter without spherical trigonometry.
