Section 14 · Summary
====================

Inverse Construction for Southern Ecliptic Parallels
----------------------------------------------------

1. **Transition to the Aranea (Rete)**:
   Having completed rising times using parallels to the equator, Ptolemy turns to circles parallel to the zodiac (ecliptic latitude circles) required to plot fixed stars on the astrolabe's *aranea* (spider).

2. **Recovering the Equinoctial Circle from the Outer Rim**:
   Given the outer bounding southern parallel :math:`abgd` (radius :math:`R = ed`) and southern declination arc :math:`dz`, constructing :math:`gh \parallel ed`, :math:`ht \perp ed`, and :math:`dkg` determines :math:`tk = em`. By parallelogram :math:`hgmd`, :math:`\angle gme = \angle zde`, so inner circle :math:`clm` of radius :math:`el = tk` recovers the equinoctial at angular distance :math:`gz \sim ln`.
