Section 15 · Summary
====================

Ecliptic Pole & Orthogonal Great Circles
----------------------------------------

1. **Stereographic Projection of the Ecliptic Pole**:
   With equinoctial circle :math:`abgd` (center :math:`e`), zodiac :math:`lbhd`, and line of centers :math:`lahg`, cutting arc :math:`bt = \varepsilon` (the obliquity separating the equatorial and ecliptic poles) and drawing :math:`dkt` locates the projected pole of the zodiac at :math:`k` on the colure diameter.

2. **Circles of Ecliptic Longitude**:
   Any great circle passing through the ecliptic pole :math:`k` and two diametrically opposite points of the zodiac :math:`lbhd` also bisects the equinoctial circle :math:`abgd`. By Section 3, every circle bisecting one great circle bisects the other, cutting both orthogonally as an ecliptic meridian (circle of longitude).
