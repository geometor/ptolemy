Section 13 · Summary
====================

Oblique Ascensions of the Remaining Signs (Clima of Rhodes)
-----------------------------------------------------------

1. **Two-Sign Ascensional Difference (Pisces + Aquarius)**:
   Using the parallel radius :math:`et = 86^p 29' 42''` at the head of Aquarius, division into :math:`3600` yields :math:`ey = 22^p 25' 42''` (scaled to :math:`32^p 36'` for :math:`ec = 120^p`). Half the corresponding arc gives the ascensional difference :math:`\Delta\alpha = 15^\circ 46'`.

2. **Individual Sign Rising Times (Clima IV, Rhodes)**:
   From the *sphaera recta* two-sign rising :math:`57^\circ 44'` minus :math:`15^\circ 46'` comes :math:`41^\circ 58'` (Pisces + Aquarius), yielding Aquarius :math:`= 22^\circ 46'` and Taurus :math:`= 22^\circ 46'`. Adding :math:`15^\circ 46'` gives :math:`73^\circ 30'` (Leo + Virgo), yielding Leo :math:`= 37^\circ 2'` and Scorpio :math:`= 37^\circ 2'`. Quadrant complements yield Gemini/Capricorn :math:`= 29^\circ 17'` and Cancer/Sagittarius :math:`= 35^\circ 15'`.
