Section 13 · Notes
==================

Clima of Rhodes & Transmission Apparatus
----------------------------------------

1. **The Fourth Clima (Island of Rhodes)**:
   Ptolemy computes the oblique ascensions for the fourth clima (Rhodes, latitude :math:`36^\circ` N, longest day :math:`14^h 30^m`) as the central paradigm of the seven habitable climata, matching the rising-time tables in *Almagest* II.8.

2. **Textual & Diagram Witnesses**:
   The Heiberg figure on p. 248 labels points :g:`z, t, a, c, m, y, b, e, d, h, l, g, n`. In the critical apparatus on p. 249, manuscript A preserves a gloss noting the Arabic term *zernen* / *zemenen* (*zaman*, time) behind Hermann's rendering *spatiis temporis*.
