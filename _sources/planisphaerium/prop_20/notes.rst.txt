Section 20 · Notes
==================

Practical Astrolabe Graduations & Toulouse Colophon
---------------------------------------------------

1. **Alexandrian Anchor & Astrolabe Grid Spacings**:
   Ptolemy's closing recommendation to calibrate the ecliptic example as close as possible to Egypt (*propinquum Egipto*) reflects his observational base at Alexandria, while spacing engraved parallels at :math:`2^\circ`, :math:`3^\circ`, or :math:`6^\circ` establishes the standard *sexpartite*, *tripartite*, and *bipartite* astrolabe plates.

2. **Toulouse Colophon (1 June 1143)**:
   At the close of Section 20 on Heiberg p. 259, the critical apparatus records the colophon preserved in manuscripts A and B: *explicit liber anno domini MCXLIII kal. iunii Tolose translatus* (with variant *toleto* in B), dating :person:`Hermann of Carinthia <hermann>`'s Latin translation of the *Planisphaerium* to 1 June 1143 at Toulouse.
