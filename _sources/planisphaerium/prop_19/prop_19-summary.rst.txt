Section 19 · Summary
====================

Degenerate Projection of the Pole-Passing Ecliptic Parallel
-----------------------------------------------------------

1. **Construction of the Straight Line** :math:`bcy`:
   When a parallel to the zodiac passes directly through the south pole :math:`d` (with spherical diameter :math:`dl`), producing :math:`dl` to strike the equatorial diameter :math:`aen` at :math:`c` and erecting :math:`cb \perp aen` yields the straight line :math:`bcy`.

2. **Planar Intersection Argument**:
   Because every projection ray from the pole :math:`d` to points on this circle lies in the circle's own spatial plane, its stereographic image is the common line of intersection :math:`bcy` of that plane with the equatorial plane, orthogonal to the meridian plane on :math:`fd`.
