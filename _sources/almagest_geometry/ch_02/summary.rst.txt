Chapter 2 · Summary
===================

Plan of the Treatise and Cosmological Hypotheses
------------------------------------------------

Chapter 2 (*Heiberg I, pp. 8–10*) outlines the deductive sequence of the *Syntaxis*, proceeding from preliminary spherical cosmology to particular planetary theories:

* **Order of Inquiry**: Oblique ecliptic and terrestrial climes first, then solar and lunar motions, and finally the fixed stars and five planets.
* **Method**: Empirical phenomena and historical observations paired with geometric demonstrations.
* **Five Hypotheses**: Spherical heavens, spherical earth, central earth, point-like earth, and terrestrial immobility.
