Chapter 3 · Summary
===================

Empirical, Geometric, and Physical Proofs of Celestial Sphericity
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 3 (*Heiberg I, pp. 10–14*) establishes that the heavens are spherical and revolve spherically through four converging lines of argument:

* **Circumpolar Stars**: Ever-visible stars trace concentric circles about a fixed pole, while rising and setting stars maintain invariant intervals.
* **Refutation of Alternatives**: Rectilinear infinity, terrestrial kindling and quenching, and non-spherical figures contradict constant stellar sizes, horizon occultation, and horoscopic instruments.
* **Isoperimetry & Aether**: The sphere is most mobile, maximizes volume, and befits uniform aether.
