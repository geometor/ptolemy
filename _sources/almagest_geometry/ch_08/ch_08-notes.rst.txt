Chapter 8 · Notes
=================

Editorial & Astronomical Notes
------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 26–30 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0036``–``0040``).
* **Terminology & Apparatus Variants**: Ptolemy defines the solstitial colure through both pairs of poles, the meridian, and the four cardinal ecliptic points (vernal, autumnal, summer, winter). At p. 26 l. 4, the heading is omitted in MSS A, C, and D, supplied marginally in D.
