Chapter 14 · Notes
==================

Editorial & Computational Notes
-------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed directly from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 76–79 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0086``–``0089``).
* **Exactness of Ptolemy's Worked Examples**: On p. 78, lines 7 and 10, Heiberg reads ``κδ ιε νϛ`` (:math:`24^p 15' 56''`, with half of :math:`48;31,55` rounded down to :math:`56''` vs. :math:`57.5''` — noted in the apparatus where MSS BC read ``κζ`` and MS B margin reads ``ν``). Both examples are verified symbolically in :func:`geometor.ptolemy.compute_solar_declination`.
* **Relation to Chapter 15**: The brief closing sentence on p. 79 introduces the complete 90-degree *Table of Obliquity* (:doc:`../ch_15/ch_15-en`).
