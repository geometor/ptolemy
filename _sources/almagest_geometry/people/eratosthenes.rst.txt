Eratosthenes
============

**Eratosthenes of Cyrene (Ἐρατοσθένης, c. 276 – c. 194 BCE)**
   Alexandrian geographer and mathematician; measured the arc between the tropics as :math:`\frac{11}{83}` of the meridian circle (*Almagest* I.12).
