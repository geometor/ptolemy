Hermann
=======

**Hermann of Carinthia (Hermannus Secundus, c. 1100 – c. 1160 CE)**
   Medieval philosopher and translator who rendered Ptolemy's *Planisphaerium* from Maslama's Arabic recension into Latin at Toulouse on 1 June 1143.
