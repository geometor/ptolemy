Chapter 7 · Summary
===================

Immobility of the Earth (No Translation or Rotation)
----------------------------------------------------

Chapter 7 (*Heiberg I, pp. 21–26*) argues that the earth neither moves laterally from the cosmic center nor rotates diurnally on its axis:

* **No Lateral Displacement**: Any shift from the center reproduces Chapter 5's contradictions; radial gravity and mutual compression anchor the earth centrally.
* **No Diurnal Rotation**: Although celestial appearances alone might permit axial rotation, clouds, birds, and projectiles would perpetually lag westward—or remain motionless if carried with the air.
