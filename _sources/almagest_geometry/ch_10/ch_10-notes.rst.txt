Historical & Mathematical Analysis

1. Ptolemy's Single-Circle Pentagon vs. Euclid IV.11
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

In :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` *Elements* IV.11, inscribing a regular pentagon in a given circle proceeds indirectly: one first constructs an auxiliary golden isosceles triangle (:math:`72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}36^\circ`) outside the circle via *Elements* II.11 and IV.10, inscribes a similar triangle via IV.2, and bisects its base angles.
