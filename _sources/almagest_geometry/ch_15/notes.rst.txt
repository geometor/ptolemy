Chapter 15 · Notes
==================

Editorial & Numerical Apparatus
-------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed directly from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 80–81 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0090``–``0091``), where the two-column table is typeset in landscape orientation across two facing pages.
* **Greek Sexagesimal Notation**: Degrees (`1°`–`90°`, `α`–`ϟ`), minutes, and seconds are printed in Ionian alphabetic numerals with `ο` denoting zero (`0`) and `ϟ` (*koppa*) denoting `90`.
* **Symbolic Verification**: Every entry in this table can be checked directly via :func:`geometor.ptolemy.compute_solar_declination`, confirming Ptolemy's sexagesimal precision to within a few arcseconds across the entire quadrant.
