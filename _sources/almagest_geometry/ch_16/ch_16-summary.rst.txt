Chapter 16 · Summary
====================

Right Ascension (*Sphaera Recta* Risings)
-----------------------------------------

Chapter 16 (*Heiberg I, pp. 82–85*) closes Book I of the *Almagest* by computing the equatorial right ascension :math:`\alpha(\lambda)` corresponding to ecliptic longitude :math:`\lambda`—the exact time-degrees in which zodiacal arcs cross the meridian at every latitude and cross the horizon on the right sphere (*sphaera recta*, terrestrial equator):

.. math::

   \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\alpha)}{120} = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - 2\varepsilon)}{\operatorname{crd}(2\varepsilon)} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\delta)}{\operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - 2\delta)} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \sin\alpha = \cot\varepsilon \tan\delta.

* **First Sign** (:math:`0^\circ \to 30^\circ`): :math:`\alpha(30^\circ) = 27^\circ 50'` (broken into 10° decans: :math:`9^\circ 10'`, :math:`9^\circ 15'`, :math:`9^\circ 25'`).
* **Second Sign** (:math:`30^\circ \to 60^\circ`): :math:`\Delta\alpha = 29^\circ 54'`, cumulative :math:`\alpha(60^\circ) = 57^\circ 44'` (decans: :math:`9^\circ 40'`, :math:`9^\circ 58'`, :math:`10^\circ 16'`).
* **Third Sign** (:math:`60^\circ \to 90^\circ`): :math:`\Delta\alpha = 32^\circ 16'`, cumulative :math:`\alpha(90^\circ) = 90^\circ 0'` (decans: :math:`10^\circ 34'`, :math:`10^\circ 47'`, :math:`10^\circ 55'`).
