.. _ptolemy-glossary:

Greek & Latin Mathematical Glossary
===================================

Technical mathematical and astronomical lexicon for *Almagest* Book I and *Planisphaerium*.

.. _term-syntaxis-mathematike:

**Μαθηματικὴ Σύνταξις** (*Syntaxis Mathematica* / *Almagest*)
   Ptolemy's original Greek title ("Mathematical Systematic Treatise"); later styled *ἡ μεγίστη* ("the greatest"), whence Arabic *al-Majisṭī* and Latin *Almagestum*.

.. _term-tmema:

**τμῆμα, pl. τμήματα** (*part / unit of diameter*)
   One of the :math:`120` equal parts (:math:`120^p`) into which Ptolemy divides the diameter of the reference circle (*Almagest* I.10), making the radius :math:`R = 60^p`.

.. _term-moira:

**μοῖρα, pl. μοῖραι** (*degree of arc*)
   One of the :math:`360` equal divisions (:math:`360^\circ`) of the circle's circumference.

.. _term-hexekoston:

**ἑξηκοστόν (πρῶτον, δεύτερον)** (*sexagesimal sixtieth*)
   Positional base-60 fractional place (:math:`p; a, b = p + \frac{a}{60} + \frac{b}{3600}`) replacing awkward Greek unit fractions (`μοναριασμοί`).

.. _term-eutheia-hypoteinousa:

**εὐθεῖα ὑποτείνουσα περιφέρειαν** (*chord subtending an arc*, :math:`\operatorname{crd}\theta`)
   The straight line joining the endpoints of an arc :math:`\theta` on a circle of diameter :math:`D = 120^p`: :math:`\operatorname{crd}(\theta) = 120\sin(\theta/2)`.

.. _term-akron-kai-meson-logon:

**ἄκρον καὶ μέσον λόγον** (*extreme and mean ratio* / golden section :math:`\phi`)
   Division of a segment :math:`\Gamma Z` at :math:`\Delta` such that :math:`\Gamma Z : \Gamma\Delta = \Gamma\Delta : \Delta Z` (*Elements* VI.Def.3); in *Almagest* I.10, :math:`\Gamma\Delta = R = 60^p` and :math:`\Delta Z = R/\phi = s_{10}`.

.. _term-tetragonon-eggegrammenon:

**τετράπλευρον ἐν κύκλῳ ἐγγεγραμμένον** (*cyclic quadrilateral*)
   A four-sided figure inscribed in a circle; governed by Ptolemy's Theorem (:math:`AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot DA`).

.. _term-dia-ton-grammon:

**διὰ τῶν γραμμῶν** (*by means of lines*)
   Exact geometric construction; Ptolemy notes (*Almagest* I.10, p. 42) that trisecting :math:`1\frac{1}{2}^\circ` to obtain :math:`\operatorname{crd}(\frac{1}{2}^\circ)` cannot be achieved `διὰ τῶν γραμμῶν`.

.. _term-kata-synthesin:

**κατὰ σύνθεσιν** (*by composition*)
   First form of the Menelaus compound-ratio theorem (*Almagest* I.13, p. 69): :math:`\frac{\Gamma A}{AE} = \frac{\Gamma\Delta}{\Delta Z}\cdot\frac{ZB}{BE}`.

.. _term-kata-diairesin:

**κατὰ διαίρεσιν** (*by division / separation*)
   Second form of the Menelaus compound-ratio theorem (*Almagest* I.13, p. 69): :math:`\frac{\Gamma E}{EA} = \frac{\Gamma Z}{Z\Delta}\cdot\frac{\Delta B}{BA}`.

.. _term-loxosis:

**λόξωσις τοῦ διὰ μέσων τῶν ζωδίων κύκλου** (*obliquity of the ecliptic*, :math:`\varepsilon`)
   Angle between the celestial equator and ecliptic; measured in *Almagest* I.12 as :math:`\varepsilon = \frac{11}{166} \times 360^\circ \approx 23^\circ 51'20''`.

.. _term-planisphaerium:

**Planisphaerium** (*Ἅπλωσις ἐπιφανείας σφαίρας*)
   Ptolemy's treatise on stereographic projection of the celestial sphere onto the equatorial plane.

.. _term-circulus-declivis:

**circulus declivis / signifer** (*oblique / zodiacal circle*)
   Latin term in *Planisphaerium* §1 for the projected ecliptic circle `mbtd` bisecting the equator at `b` and `d`.

.. _term-subcontraria-sectio:

**ὑπεναντία τομή / sectio subcontraria** (*subcontrary section*)
   Anti-parallel plane section of an oblique circular cone (*Apollonius Conics* I.5), proving that circles on :math:`S^2` map to circles under stereographic projection.

.. _term-almucantar:

**almucantar** (*al-muqanṭarāt*)
   Small circle of constant altitude :math:`h` above the horizon, projected stereographically onto an eccentric circle on the astrolabe plate.
