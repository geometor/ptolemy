Conspectus Operis
-----------------

Composed at Alexandria around 150 CE by :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>` and dedicated to :person:`Syrus <syrus>`, the opening fourteen chapters of the :term:`Syntaxis Mathematica <syntaxis-mathematike>` (*Almagest*) and its companion treatise :term:`Planisphaerium <planisphaerium>` constitute the supreme surviving synthesis of classical Greek computational geometry, spherical trigonometry, and conformal cartographic projection. Each unit of this **Multi-Track Book Codex** provides synchronized reading tracks (**English**, **Greek (1898)** or **Latin**, **Summary**, and **Notes**) wired to interactive `.euclid-graphic` figures:

* **Unit I** (*Almagest I.1–I.9 & I.10a · Heiberg I, pp. 4–35*): :doc:`Cosmic Preliminaries & Golden Pentagon–Decagon Construction <unit-1-pentagon-decagon/unit-1-pentagon-decagon-en>` — Theoretical philosophy, spherical heavens and central Earth, the sexagesimal circle ($360^\circ$, $D = 120^p$, $R = 60^p$), and Ptolemy's single-circle compass construction of the regular decagon ($s_{10} = R/\phi = 37;4,55^p$) and regular pentagon ($s_5 = \sqrt{R^2 + s_{10}^2} = 70;32,3^p$). (:doc:`EN <unit-1-pentagon-decagon/unit-1-pentagon-decagon-en>` · :doc:`EL <unit-1-pentagon-decagon/unit-1-pentagon-decagon-el>` · :doc:`Summary <unit-1-pentagon-decagon/unit-1-pentagon-decagon-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <unit-1-pentagon-decagon/unit-1-pentagon-decagon-notes>`)
* **Unit II** (*Almagest I.10b · Heiberg I, pp. 36–46*): :doc:`Ptolemy's Theorem on Cyclic Quadrilaterals & Chord Calculus <unit-2-ptolemy-chords/unit-2-ptolemy-chords-en>` — Complete proof of Ptolemy's Theorem ($AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot DA$), the Chord of Difference ($\operatorname{crd} 12^\circ$), the Half-Arc Chord Lemma ($6^\circ \to 3^\circ \to 1\frac{1}{2}^\circ \to \frac{3}{4}^\circ$), the Chord of Sum, impossibility of trisecting $1\frac{1}{2}^\circ$ :term:`by means of lines <dia-ton-grammon>`, and **Ptolemy's Inequality Lemma** ($\operatorname{crd} 1^\circ \approx 1;2,50 \implies \pi \approx 3;8,30 = \frac{377}{120}$). (:doc:`EN <unit-2-ptolemy-chords/unit-2-ptolemy-chords-en>` · :doc:`EL <unit-2-ptolemy-chords/unit-2-ptolemy-chords-el>` · :doc:`Summary <unit-2-ptolemy-chords/unit-2-ptolemy-chords-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <unit-2-ptolemy-chords/unit-2-ptolemy-chords-notes>`)
* **Unit III** (*Almagest I.11–I.14 · Heiberg I, pp. 47–78*): :doc:`Table of Chords, Ecliptic Obliquity & Menelaus Sphaerica <unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica/unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica-en>` — Architecture and self-checking of the Table of Chords, meridian measurement of $2\varepsilon = \frac{11}{83}$ of a circle ($47^\circ 42'40''$), the **Planar Menelaus Sector Lemma** (*kata synthesin* & *kata diairesin*), the **3D Spherical Menelaus Theorem**, and the **Solar Declination Law** ($\operatorname{crd} 2\delta = \operatorname{crd} 2\varepsilon \cdot \operatorname{crd} 2\lambda / 120$). (:doc:`EN <unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica/unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica-en>` · :doc:`EL <unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica/unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica-el>` · :doc:`Summary <unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica/unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica/unit-3-menelaus-sphaerica-notes>`)
* **Unit IV** (*Planisphaerium §§1–4 · Heiberg II, pp. 227–234*): :doc:`Planisphaerium: Stereographic Projection & Astrolabe Geometry <unit-4-planisphaerium/unit-4-planisphaerium-en>` — South-Pole stereographic projection onto the equatorial plane, the Equator ($abgd$), Tropics ($tl, km$), and eccentric Ecliptic circle ($mbtd$) bisecting the equator via $\angle mdt = 90^\circ$, :person:`Apollonius's <apollonius>` *Conics* I.5 subcontrary cone section, and circular inversion ($ez : ed = ed : ek$, $92;8,15 \times 39;4,19 \approx 60^2$). (:doc:`EN <unit-4-planisphaerium/unit-4-planisphaerium-en>` · :doc:`LA <unit-4-planisphaerium/unit-4-planisphaerium-la>` · :doc:`Summary <unit-4-planisphaerium/unit-4-planisphaerium-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <unit-4-planisphaerium/unit-4-planisphaerium-notes>`)

Editorial & Translation Note
----------------------------

All primary polytonic Greek (*Almagest*) and medieval scientific Latin (*Planisphaerium*) texts in this codex are transcribed directly via multimodal page-scan inspection from :person:`J. L. Heiberg's <heiberg>` public-domain Teubner critical editions (:cite:`Heiberg 1898 <heiberg:1898>`; :cite:`Heiberg 1907 <heiberg:1907>`), cross-checked against the public-domain editions of :person:`N. B. Halma <halma>` (:cite:`1813 <halma:1813>`) and :person:`K. Manitius <manitius>` (:cite:`1912 <manitius:1912>`). Every English translation (`-en`), summary (`-summary`), and commentary (`-notes`) in this repository is **100% originally authored for GEOMETOR** directly from Heiberg's Greek and Latin; no copyrighted modern English translations were consulted or reproduced.
