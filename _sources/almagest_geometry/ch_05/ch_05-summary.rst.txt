Chapter 5 · Summary
===================

Geocentric Station of the Earth
-------------------------------

Chapter 5 (*Heiberg I, pp. 16–20*) proves by elimination that the earth lies at the center of the celestial sphere, refuting three alternatives:

* **Off-axis, equidistant from the poles**: Displacements toward zenith, nadir, east, or west destroy equinoctial symmetry and equal rising-to-culmination times.
* **On-axis, displaced toward one pole**: Unequal horizons at inclined latitudes would prevent bisection of the ecliptic and misalign equinoctial gnomon shadows.
* **Neither on-axis nor equidistant**: Combines both classes of contradiction.

Finally, lunar eclipses occur exclusively at diametrical opposition to the sun, confirming central placement.
