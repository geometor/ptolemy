Chapter 5 · Notes
=================

Editorial & Cosmological Notes
------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 16–20 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0026``–``0030``).
* **Observational & Textual Notes**: The simultaneous visibility of six zodiacal signs above the horizon and collinear equinoctial gnomon shadows establish that every horizon plane bisects the ecliptic. At p. 18 l. 16, MS A reads ``διαφθόρως`` where Heiberg adopts ``διαφόρως``.
