Chapter 6 · Notes
=================

Editorial & Observational Notes
-------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 20–21 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0030``–``0031``).
* **Observational Scope & Variants**: The point-ratio holds to sense perception relative to the fixed-star sphere; perceptible lunar and solar parallax are deferred to Book V. At p. 20 l. 3, chapter numerals are omitted in MSS A, C, and D, supplied marginally in D.
