Chapter 13 · Notes¶

Historical & Projective Analysis¶

1. Why One Theorem Sufficed for All Spherical Trigonometry¶

Modern spherical trigonometry relies on multiple triangle laws (spherical law of cosines, law of sines, Napier’s rules for right spherical triangles). Throughout Books I, II, and VIII of the Almagest—computing solar declinations (I.14), right ascensions (I.16), ascensional differences, oblique ascensions, length of daylight, meridian shadows, and rising/setting stars (Book II)—Ptolemy uses only a single spherical theorem: the six-quantity Menelaus transversal configuration of Chapter 13 (Menelaus, Sphaerica III.1).

By choosing three quadrant arcs ($90^\circ$, whose double-arc chord is the diameter $120^p$) in the six-arc Menelaus figure, three of the six chords collapse to $120^p$, instantly producing every right-spherical-triangle identity:

  • $\sin\delta = \sin\varepsilon\sin\lambda$ (Almagest I.14, Declination)

  • $\tan\alpha = \cos\varepsilon\tan\lambda$ (Almagest I.16, Right Ascension)

  • $\sin\Delta\alpha = \tan\varphi\tan\delta$ (Almagest II.7, Ascensional Difference / Rising Time)

2. Projective Essence of Ptolemy’s 3D Proof (Eucl. XI.3)¶

In Ptolemy’s proof on page 75 of Heiberg: rather than working intrinsically on the curved sphere, Ptolemy projects the spherical figure from the center $H$ of the sphere onto the flat plane containing $\triangle A\Gamma\Delta$. Under central (gnomonic) projection from $H$, every great circle on the sphere is the intersection of a central plane with the sphere, and therefore projects onto the plane $\Pi(A,\Gamma,\Delta)$ as a straight line (Eucl. XI.3). Thus the great-circle transversal $B\text{–}Z\text{–}E$ becomes the straight planar Menelaus line $\Lambda\text{–}K\text{–}\Theta$!

In geometor.ptolemy.menelaus_sphaerica.verify_spherical_menelaus(), we instantiate six exact rational points on the 3D sphere $X^2 + Y^2 + Z^2 = 9$ (A = (0,0,3), B = (2,-1,2), C = (1,2,2), etc.), compute the exact 3D line-plane intersections $\Theta, K, \Lambda \in \mathbb{Q}^3$, verify $\Theta - \Lambda \parallel K - \Lambda$ ($\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \vec{0}$), and confirm exact equality of squared double-arc chord ratios ($\frac{1089}{625} = \frac{1089}{625}$).
