Chapter 13 · Summary¶

The Planar & Spherical Menelaus Lemmas¶

  1. Planar Menelaus Sector Lemmas (Heiberg I, pp. 68–70): Drawing an auxiliary parallel establishes both compound ratios by composition (κατὰ σύνθεσιν) and by division (κατὰ διαίρεσιν):

    \[\frac{\Gamma A}{AE} = \frac{\Gamma\Delta}{\Delta Z} \cdot \frac{ZB}{BE}, \qquad \frac{\Gamma E}{EA} = \frac{\Gamma Z}{\Delta Z} \cdot \frac{\Delta B}{BA}.\]

  2. Circular Chord-Ratio Lemmas & Corollaries (Heiberg I, pp. 70–74): Two auxiliary circle lemmas relate chord-ratios of double arcs to straight-line segments cut by an internal or external secant, and each lemma yields a constructive corollary determining the individual arcs whenever the total arc and the double-arc chord ratio are given:

    \[\frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 AB)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 B\Gamma)} = \frac{AE}{E\Gamma}, \qquad \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Gamma A)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 AB)} = \frac{\Gamma E}{EB}.\]

  3. 3D Central Projection & Spherical Menelaus Theorem (Heiberg I, pp. 74–76): Projecting great-circle intersections from sphere center \(H\) onto the plane of \(\triangle A\Gamma\Delta\) maps great circle \(BZE\) to straight line \(\Theta K\Lambda\) in space (Eucl. XI.3), establishing both spherical compound chord ratios:

    \[\frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Gamma E)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 EA)} = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Gamma Z)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 Z\Delta)} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Delta B)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 BA)}, \qquad \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Gamma A)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 EA)} = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Gamma\Delta)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 \Delta Z)} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2 ZB)}{\operatorname{crd}(2 BE)}.\]
