Chapter 10 · Summary¶

The Deductive Architecture of Ptolemy’s Chord Calculus (Almagest I.10)¶

  1. Single-Circle Compass Construction of the Golden Decagon & Pentagon (pp. 31–35): In semicircle $AB\Gamma$ on diameter $A\Delta\Gamma$ with $\Delta B \perp A\Gamma$ and $E$ bisecting $\Delta\Gamma$, swinging $EB = 30\sqrt{5}$ onto $\Delta A$ at $Z$ yields in a single right triangle $\triangle B\Delta Z$:

    $$\Delta Z = s_{10} = \frac{R}{\phi} = 30(\sqrt{5} - 1) \approx 37;4,55^p, \qquad BZ = s_5 = \sqrt{R^2 + s_{10}^2} = 30\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}} \approx 70;32,3^p.$$

  2. Ptolemy’s Theorem on Cyclic Quadrilaterals (pp. 36–37): In any cyclic quadrilateral $AB\Gamma\Delta$, setting $\angle ABE = \angle \Delta B\Gamma$ creates two pairs of similar triangles ($\triangle ABE \sim \triangle \Delta B\Gamma$ and $\triangle E B\Gamma \sim \triangle AB\Delta$), yielding:

    $$A\Gamma \cdot B\Delta = AB \cdot \Delta\Gamma + A\Delta \cdot B\Gamma.$$

  3. Difference & Sum Laws (pp. 38, 41–42): When one diagonal is the diameter $D = 120^p$, Ptolemy’s Theorem transforms directly into the difference formula:

    $$\operatorname{crd}(\alpha - \beta) = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(\alpha)\operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - \beta) - \operatorname{crd}(\beta)\operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - \alpha)}{120}.$$

    Applied to $72^\circ$ (pentagon) and $60^\circ$ (hexagon), this yields $\operatorname{crd}(12^\circ) \approx 12;32,36^p$.

  4. Half-Arc Chord Law (pp. 39–40): Bisecting arc $B\Gamma$ at $\Delta$ on semicircle $AB\Gamma$ gives:

    $$\operatorname{crd}^2(\theta / 2) = 60\bigl(120 - \operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - \theta)\bigr).$$

    Repeated bisection extracts $6^\circ, 3^\circ, 1\frac{1}{2}^\circ, \frac{3}{4}^\circ$.

  5. Irreducibility of Angle Trisection (p. 42): Ptolemy explicitly states that $\operatorname{crd}(\frac{1}{2}^\circ)$ cannot be constructed διὰ τῶν γραμμῶν (“by means of lines”) from $\operatorname{crd}(1\frac{1}{2}^\circ)$—anticipating the algebraic cubic irreducibility of angle trisection.

  6. Ptolemy’s Inequality Lemma & the Squeeze of $\operatorname{crd}(1^\circ)$ (pp. 43–46): Proving $\frac{\operatorname{crd}\alpha}{\operatorname{crd}\beta} < \frac{\alpha}{\beta}$ for $\alpha > \beta$ traps $\operatorname{crd}(1^\circ)$ between:

    $$\frac{2}{3}\operatorname{crd}\left(1\frac{1}{2}^\circ\right) = 1;2,50,0^p < \operatorname{crd}(1^\circ) < \frac{4}{3}\operatorname{crd}\left(\frac{3}{4}^\circ\right) = 1;2,50,40^p.$$

    This establishes $\operatorname{crd}(1^\circ) \approx 1;2,50^p$, halving to $\operatorname{crd}(\frac{1}{2}^\circ) \approx 0;31,25^p$.
