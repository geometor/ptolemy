Chapter 16 · Summary¶

Right Ascension (Sphaera Recta Risings)¶

Chapter 16 (Heiberg I, pp. 82–85) closes Book I of the Almagest by computing the equatorial right ascension \(\alpha(\lambda)\) corresponding to ecliptic longitude \(\lambda\)—the exact time-degrees in which zodiacal arcs cross the meridian at every latitude and cross the horizon on the right sphere (sphaera recta, terrestrial equator):

\[\frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\alpha)}{120} = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - 2\varepsilon)}{\operatorname{crd}(2\varepsilon)} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\delta)}{\operatorname{crd}(180^\circ - 2\delta)} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \sin\alpha = \cot\varepsilon \tan\delta.\]

  • First Sign (\(0^\circ \to 30^\circ\)): \(\alpha(30^\circ) = 27^\circ 50'\) (broken into 10° decans: \(9^\circ 10'\), \(9^\circ 15'\), \(9^\circ 25'\)).

  • Second Sign (\(30^\circ \to 60^\circ\)): \(\Delta\alpha = 29^\circ 54'\), cumulative \(\alpha(60^\circ) = 57^\circ 44'\) (decans: \(9^\circ 40'\), \(9^\circ 58'\), \(10^\circ 16'\)).

  • Third Sign (\(60^\circ \to 90^\circ\)): \(\Delta\alpha = 32^\circ 16'\), cumulative \(\alpha(90^\circ) = 90^\circ 0'\) (decans: \(10^\circ 34'\), \(10^\circ 47'\), \(10^\circ 55'\)).
