Chapter 11 · Summary¶

Structure & Computational Logic of the Table of Chords¶

  1. Table Organization: - Full extent: \(0^\circ 30'\) through \(180^\circ 00'\) in uniform half-degree increments (360 rows across 16 printed pages, folios 0058–0073, grouped into 8 classical 45-line panels). - Base parameters: Circle circumference \(= 360^\circ\), diameter \(D = 120^p\), radius \(R = 60^p\).

  2. Column Structure: - Arcs (περιφερειῶν): Central arc \(\theta\) in degrees and half-degrees (\(\angle' = 30'\)). - Chords (εὐθειῶν): Subtending chord \(\operatorname{crd}(\theta) = 120 \sin(\theta/2)\) expressed in sexagesimal parts, primes, and seconds (\(p;\,m,s\)). - Sixtieths (ἑξηκοστῶν): One-thirtieth of the half-degree difference \(\delta = \frac{1}{30}[\operatorname{crd}(\theta + 30') - \operatorname{crd}(\theta)]\), giving the linear increment per arc-minute (\(1'\)) to sexagesimal thirds (\(0;\,p_1,p_2,p_3\)).

  3. Interpolation Algorithm: For any intermediate arc \(\theta + \Delta\) with \(0 < \Delta < 30'\):

    \[\operatorname{crd}(\theta + \Delta) \approx \operatorname{crd}(\theta) + \Delta \cdot \delta.\]

    Because the second derivative \(\operatorname{crd}''(\theta) = -30\sin(\theta/2)\) remains small across a \(30'\) interval, linear interpolation has maximum error bounded by:

    \[\text{Error} \le \frac{1}{8}(\Delta\theta)^2 \cdot \max|\operatorname{crd}''| \approx \frac{1}{8}\left(\frac{\pi}{360}\right)^2 \times 30 \approx 2.8 \times 10^{-4}\text{ parts},\]

    comparable to a single sexagesimal second (\(1'' = \frac{1}{3600} \approx 2.78 \times 10^{-4}\)), preserving two-place sexagesimal accuracy across the entire semicircle.
