Section 9 · Summary
===================

Right-Sphere Ascensions: Second and Third Signs
-----------------------------------------------

Extending Section 8 to :math:`\lambda = 60^\circ` from the equinoxes using the Section 5 southern radius :math:`ek = 86;29,42^p`:

- Excess :math:`kf - fe = 3600 / 86;29,42 = 41;38,18^p`, leaving :math:`2fe = 44;51,24^p` and :math:`fe = 22;25,42^p`.
- Rescaled to :math:`et = 120^p`: :math:`fe = 101;28^p = \operatorname{crd}(115^\circ 28')`, so :math:`\alpha(60^\circ) = 57^\circ 44'`.

- **First sign** (Pisces, Aries, Virgo, Libra): :math:`27^\circ 50'`.
- **Second sign** (Aquarius, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio): :math:`57^\circ 44' - 27^\circ 50' = 29^\circ 54'`.
- **Third sign** (Gemini, Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn): :math:`90^\circ - 57^\circ 44' = 32^\circ 16'`.
- Quadrant sum: :math:`27^\circ 50' + 29^\circ 54' + 32^\circ 16' = 90^\circ 0'`.
