Section 9 · Notes
=================

Comparison with Section 5 & *Almagest* I.16
-------------------------------------------

1. **Sexagesimal Division Variant**:
   In Section 5, Ptolemy gave the northern parallel radius at :math:`30^\circ` from the solstices as :math:`41^p 39' 15''`, whereas here he obtains :math:`kf - fe = 3600 / 86;29,42 = 41^p 38' 18''` (exact value :math:`41;37,15^p`). Despite the tiny discrepancy in seconds, the resulting angle :math:`57^\circ 44'` is accurate to the nearest arcminute.

2. **Exact Agreement with *Almagest* II.8**:
   In the *Almagest* right-sphere ascension table (*Almagest* II.8), the cumulative ascensions from the equinoxes are :math:`27^\circ 50'`, :math:`57^\circ 44'`, and :math:`90^\circ 0'`, giving individual sign ascensions of :math:`27^\circ 50'`, :math:`29^\circ 54'`, and :math:`32^\circ 16'`—matching the *Planisphaerium* to the minute.
