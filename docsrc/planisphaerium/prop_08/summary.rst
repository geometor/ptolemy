Section 8 · Summary
===================

Right-Sphere Ascensions: First 30° from the Equinoxes
-----------------------------------------------------

In the right sphere (*sphaera recta*), horizons are meridian lines passing through the pole :math:`e`. By symmetry, the four zodiacal signs adjacent to the equinoctial points—Pisces, Aries, Virgo, and Libra—all share the same right ascension :math:`\alpha(30^\circ)`.

- Given radii: :math:`tk = 65;36,17^p`, :math:`et = 26;31,58^p`, and :math:`ek = 73;39,7^p` (from Section 6).
- Orthogonally projecting :math:`tf \perp ke`: :math:`kf - fe = (tk^2 - te^2)/ek = 3600 / 73;39,7 = 48;52,42^p` (:math:`= en`).
- Hence :math:`fe = \frac{1}{2}(ek - en) = 12;23,12^p`.
- In right triangle :math:`fet`: :math:`120 \cdot fe / et \approx 55;59^p = \operatorname{crd}(55^\circ 40')`, giving :math:`\alpha(30^\circ) = \angle bek = \angle fte = 27^\circ 50'`.
