Section 20 · Summary
====================

Taxonomy of Projected Circles & Practical Graduations
-----------------------------------------------------

1. **Equatorial vs. Ecliptic Projection Symmetries**:
   The equatorial pole acts as center for the equator and all its parallels (northern shrinking, southern expanding), while meridians project as concurrent straight lines. Conversely, the ecliptic pole is non-concentric with the zodiac and its parallels (one parallel becoming a straight line), and only the solstitial colure projects as a straight line.

2. **Coordinate Placement & Astrolabe Grid Intervals**:
   Fixed stars may be plotted using either equatorial or ecliptic coordinates, choosing an example close to Egypt for the ecliptic parallels. On a medium-sized instrument, engraving circles at intervals of :math:`2^\circ`, :math:`3^\circ`, or :math:`6^\circ` suffices, dividing evenly into both the :math:`30^\circ` sign arcs and the :math:`24^\circ` tropical zone.
