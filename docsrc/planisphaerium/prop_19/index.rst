Section 19 · A Parallel to the Zodiac Passing Through the South Pole
====================================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 19 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 257–258 <heiberg:1907>`)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0467
   :page: p. 257

By the example of a similar description, nevertheless, a circle parallel to the zodiac can also be conceived—that which lies upon diameter :g:`dl`, so that we produce :g:`dl` to the latitude we stated and draw it out to point :g:`c`, then from point :g:`c` draw line :g:`cb` perpendicular to line :g:`aen`,
which line holds in the planisphere the place of the circle whose diameter is :g:`dl`, since all straight lines drawn from point :g:`d` in place of

   :page: p. 258

these circles lie in the same plane as the plane of the circle; the common intersection of which plane and the plane of the equinoctial circle is line :g:`bcy`. And the plane of the meridian circle, which stands upon line :g:`fd`, is likewise orthogonal to both of those planes.
