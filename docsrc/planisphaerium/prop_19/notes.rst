Section 19 · Notes
==================

Circles Through the Projection Pole & Maslama's Scholia
-------------------------------------------------------

1. **Degenerate Circle Case in Stereographic Projection**:
   Any circle on the sphere passing through the projection pole :g:`d` has a plane containing :g:`d`, so all projection rays lie within that plane. Its stereographic image is therefore a straight line (:g:`bcy`), specifically the ecliptic parallel of latitude :math:`-(90^\circ - \varepsilon) = -66^\circ 8' 40''` (anticipated in Section 20 as *unus eorum sine centro*).

2. **Apparatus Note on Maslama's Addition**:
   On Heiberg p. 258, line 1, the apparatus again records that Heiberg omitted elements of the manuscript figure deriving from :person:`Maslama al-Majrīṭī <maslama>`'s commentary (*In fig. omisi, quae ad notam Maslemi referuntur*), referring to the shared plate figure printed on p. 257.
