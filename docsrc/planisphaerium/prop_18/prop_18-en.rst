Section 18 · Parallels to the Zodiac Intersecting Perpetually Invisible Circles
===============================================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 18 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 255–257 <heiberg:1907>`)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0465
   :page: p. 255

Next, when a circle parallel to the zodiac—neither described in the planisphere nor yet marked on the sphere—has its portion in the invisible region cutting circles parallel to the right circle that are invisible near the south pole,
whose distance from the zodiac is either less than the altitude of the head of Cancer in a definite place or less than the altitude of the head of Capricorn in a determinate place, we set the meridian circle :g:`abgd` around center :g:`e`. We shall therefore understand point :g:`d` as the south pole, axis :g:`db`,
the diameter of the equinoctial circle :g:`ag`, the diameter of the circle parallel to it that is never

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0466
   :page: p. 256

visible as line :g:`zh`, and the diameter of the circle cutting it among the parallels to the zodiac as line :g:`tkl`. These being so placed, we draw semicircle :g:`zmh` upon line :g:`zh` and raise a line from point :g:`k` to :g:`m` parallel to line :g:`de`.
From which therefore, if we draw lines :g:`agn` and :g:`dhn` as well as :g:`dlc`, the circle described at length :g:`en` through points :g:`n`, :g:`y`, :g:`q` will be one of the circles perpetually denied to the planisphere. And the circle described in place of the circle upon line :g:`tkl` must pass through point :g:`c`,
cutting circle :g:`nyq` into arcs similar to arcs :g:`hm` and :g:`mz`, since line :g:`km` is

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0467
   :page: p. 257

a common mean proportional to their surfaces. Let :g:`f` therefore join with :g:`m`, and at point :g:`e` upon line :g:`ea` let an angle be made equal to angle :g:`∠mfk`, which is angle :g:`∠aey`;
whence the line produced to point :g:`y` shows arc :g:`yq` similar to arc :g:`mz`. Thus the circle is designated in place of the circle upon line :g:`tkl` parallel to the zodiac, whose distance from the equinoctial is in the quantity of arc :g:`gl`,
cutting the perpetually hidden parallels to the right circle with such similarity. This circle must be understood in the figure as passing through :g:`c` and :g:`y` to reach opposite point :g:`o`, where :g:`dt` and :g:`ea` produced straight meet just as :g:`dh` and :g:`eg` meet at point :g:`n`.
