Section 17 · Summary
====================

Non-Concentricity of Ecliptic Parallels
---------------------------------------

1. **Setup & Auxiliary Circumcircle**:
   Two parallel circles to the zodiac with meridian diameters :math:`zh` and :math:`tk` project onto equatorial diameters :math:`lm` (midpoint :math:`o`) and :math:`nc` respectively. Circumscribing circle :math:`dyf` about :math:`\triangle dnc` yields :math:`yn = cf` (since :math:`zt = kh`), making :math:`lm \parallel fy`.

2. **Inequality of Midpoints via Power of a Point**:
   From :math:`dl : ly = dm : mf` and intersecting chords (:math:`dl \cdot ly = cl \cdot ln`, :math:`dm \cdot mf = nm \cdot cm`), it follows that :math:`dl^2 : dm^2 = (cl \cdot ln) : (nm \cdot cm)`. Since :math:`dm > dl`, we have :math:`(nc + cm) \cdot cm > (nc + nl) \cdot nl`, so :math:`cm > nl` and :math:`oc < on`.
