Section 17 · Notes
==================

Non-Concentricity & Textual Apparatus
-------------------------------------

1. **Conformal Distortion of Centers**:
   Although stereographic projection maps every circle on the sphere to a circle (or line) on the plane, it preserves centers only for parallels centered on the axis of projection (the equator). Any family of parallel small circles centered around an inclined pole such as the ecliptic pole projects into a non-concentric nested pencil.

2. **Diagram & Apparatus Notes**:
   The figure on Heiberg p. 254 depicts points :g:`d, y, a, l, n, e, o, c, m, f, g, h, k, z, t, b`. On p. 255, line 8, Heiberg follows Federico Commandino's 1558 emendation *ex cl et ln* where manuscripts ACDE read *cl ex* and B reads *dl ex*.
