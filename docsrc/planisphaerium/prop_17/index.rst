Section 17 · Non-Concentricity of Projected Parallels to the Zodiac
===================================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 17 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 254–255 <heiberg:1907>`)

Circles parallel to the zodiac designated in this manner must always have different centers. For let there be as before the meridian circle :g:`abgd` around center :g:`e`, axis line :g:`bed`, diameter of the equinoctial circle line :g:`ag`, and diameters of circles parallel to the zodiac lines :g:`zh` and :g:`tk`;
and let lines :g:`dlz`, :g:`dmh`, :g:`dnt`, and :g:`dck` be drawn. Next we describe circle :g:`dyf` around triangle :g:`dnc`, and having drawn line :g:`yf`, we divide line :g:`lm` into halves at point :g:`o`. Since therefore it is certain that the circle whose diameter is :g:`zh` can be described about diameter :g:`lm`,
and thus the circle whose diameter is :g:`tk` about diameter :g:`nc`, **I say that** these two circles are by no means of the same center—that is, point :g:`o` is not at all the midpoint on diameter :g:`nc`. For since arc :g:`zt` equals arc :g:`kh`, arc :g:`yn` will also equal arc :g:`cf`; whence lines :g:`lm` and :g:`fy` are parallel.
Therefore, the ratio of line :g:`dl` to line :g:`ly` is the same as that of line :g:`dm` to :g:`mf`. Moreover,

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0465
   :page: p. 255

the ratio of line :g:`dl` to :g:`ly` is the same as that of the square on :g:`dl` to the rectangle contained by :g:`dl` and :g:`ly`, and the ratio of :g:`dm` to line :g:`mf` is the same as that of the square on :g:`dm` to the rectangle contained by :g:`dm` and :g:`mf`.
Since therefore, by reason of the circle, :g:`dl` by :g:`ly` equals :g:`lc` by :g:`ln`, and likewise :g:`dm` by :g:`mf` equals :g:`nm` by :g:`cm`, the ratio of the square on :g:`dl` to :g:`cl` by :g:`ln` will be the same as that of the square on :g:`dm` to :g:`mn` by :g:`cm`;
alternately, therefore, the ratio of the square on :g:`dl` to the square on :g:`dm` is the same as that of the rectangle produced from :g:`cl` and :g:`ln` to the rectangle constituted from :g:`nm` and :g:`cm`. Now the square on :g:`dm` is greater than the square on :g:`dl`, just as :g:`dm` is longer than :g:`dl`;
so therefore :g:`nm` by :g:`cm` is greater than :g:`cl` by :g:`ln`. Since therefore the common middle segment :g:`nc` with :g:`cm` in :g:`mc` is greater than with :g:`nl` in :g:`ln`, it is clear that :g:`cm` is greater than :g:`nl`.
Now :g:`mo` is given equal to :g:`lo`; hence it follows that :g:`oc` is less than :g:`on`. Therefore point :g:`o` cannot be the midpoint on diameter :g:`nc`; and since it is given as the midpoint on diameter :g:`ml`, it is impossible for circles parallel to the zodiac to have the same center.
