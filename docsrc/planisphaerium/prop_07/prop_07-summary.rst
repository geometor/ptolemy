Section 7 · Summary
===================

Rhodes Horizon Parameters (:math:`\varphi = 36^\circ`)
------------------------------------------------------

For a terrestrial latitude :math:`\varphi = 36^\circ` (the parallel of Rhodes), the horizon is a great circle touching the circumpolar parallels at declination :math:`\pm (90^\circ - \varphi) = \pm 54^\circ`:

- Colure arcs: :math:`90^\circ + 54^\circ = 144^\circ` and :math:`90^\circ - 54^\circ = 36^\circ`.
- Chords (:math:`D = 120^p`): :math:`\operatorname{crd}(144^\circ) = 114;7,37^p` and :math:`\operatorname{crd}(36^\circ) = 37;4,55^p`.

- Extreme horizon intersections on the meridian: :math:`ez = 184^p 39' 48''` (south) and :math:`ek = 19^p 29' 42''` (north).
- Horizon diameter: :math:`ez + ek = 204^p 9' 30''`.
- Horizon radius: :math:`R_{\text{hor}} = 102^p 4' 45''` (:math:`60 / \sin 36^\circ`).
- Center offset from pole :math:`e`: :math:`d_{\text{hor}} = 82^p 35' 3''` (:math:`60 \cot 36^\circ`).
