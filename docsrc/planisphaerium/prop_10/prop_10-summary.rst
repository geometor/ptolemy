Section 10 · Summary
====================

Symmetry & Ascensional Difference in the Oblique Sphere
-------------------------------------------------------

By placing the stereographic horizon at both solstitial intersections—arcs :math:`zkhl` (centered at :math:`c`) and :math:`zmhn` (centered at :math:`y`)—the line of centers :math:`cty` bisects the solstitial diameter :math:`zh` orthogonally at :math:`t`, while :math:`ce \perp kl` and :math:`ye \perp mn`.

- Congruent triangles :math:`cet \cong yet` force :math:`\angle aek = \angle aem`, so the four ascensional-difference arcs satisfy :math:`ak = am = gn = gl`.
- Opposite quadrants around the vernal equinox (:math:`hb`, :math:`bz`) lose :math:`ak`, while those opposite them (:math:`dh`, :math:`zd`) gain :math:`gn = ak`.
- Hence longest day :math:`= 180^\circ + 2 \cdot ak` and shortest day :math:`= 180^\circ - 2 \cdot ak`.
