Planar Proof of Oblique Ascension Symmetries (*Almagest* II.7)
--------------------------------------------------------------

1. **Diurnal Rotation Convention**:
   With the north celestial pole fixed at :math:`e` and the south pole at infinity, diurnal rotation carries celestial points clockwise along circles centered at :math:`e` (:math:`d \to g \to b \to a`). Consequently, the horizon circle remains fixed while the ecliptic circle rotates around :math:`e`—or equivalently, the horizon circle takes successive positions around the fixed ecliptic diagram.

2. **Equivalence to Spherical Ascensional Difference**:
   The symmetry laws proven here purely by congruent planar triangles :math:`cet \cong yet` match *Almagest* II.7: symmetrical arcs about the equinoxes have equal oblique ascensions, opposite signs sum to twice their right ascension, and the solstitial ascensional difference :math:`ak` satisfies :math:`\sin(ak) = \tan \varphi \tan \varepsilon`.
