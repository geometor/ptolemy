Section 15 · Notes
==================

Manuscript Apparatus & Ecliptic Coordinates
-------------------------------------------

1. **Use of the Letter k in Hermann's Text**:
   On Heiberg p. 251, line 29, the phrase *circa centrum k* carries a marginal note in manuscript A (*in alio non erat circa centrum k*), while the sentence *transibit et linea per dkt, punctum uero k potentia erit polus zodiaci* (p. 252, lines 4–5) is supplied in the margin of A.

2. **Diagram & Coordinate Inversion**:
   The figure on Heiberg p. 251 labels points :g:`d, l, a, k, e, h, g, t, b`. Whereas Ptolemy's *Almagest* star catalogue tabulates fixed stars in ecliptic longitude and latitude, an astrolabe *rete* rotates around the equatorial pole :g:`e`, requiring stereographic projection of the ecliptic pole :g:`k` and its coordinate circles.
