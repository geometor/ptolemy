Section 12 · Summary
====================

Oblique Ascensions for Rhodes (:math:`\varphi = 36^\circ`): First Sign
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Applying the bisection method to the horizon passing through :math:`t` (:math:`30^\circ` from the equinox, radius :math:`et = 73;39,7^p`) and centered at :math:`c` (:math:`ec = 82;35,3^p`):

- Orthogonally projecting :math:`cy \perp tl`: :math:`ey = \frac{1}{2}(et - 3600/et) = 12;23,12^p` (printed :math:`12;22,12^p`).
- Rescaled to :math:`ec = 120^p`: :math:`ey \approx 18^p = \operatorname{crd}(17^\circ 16')`.

- Ascensional difference :math:`am = \angle tem = \angle ecy = \frac{1}{2}(17^\circ 16') = 8^\circ 38'` (printed as ``VIIII puncta XXXVIII``, i.e., :math:`9^\circ 38'`, in Hermann's Latin).
- **Aries & Pisces**: :math:`27^\circ 50' - 8^\circ 38' = 19^\circ 12'`.
- **Virgo & Libra**: :math:`27^\circ 50' + 8^\circ 38' = 36^\circ 28'`.
