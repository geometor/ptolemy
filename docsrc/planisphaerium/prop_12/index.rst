Next, therefore, in order to measure the rising of the signs in this clime, we shall likewise set up the equinoctial circle :g:`abgd` around center :g:`e`, and the zodiac :g:`hdzb`. This done, we shall cut off from the zodiac arc :g:`bt`, first to the measure of one sign—which is established to be Pisces—
and join straight line :g:`tel`, and likewise describe the horizon circle for the latitude of :math:`36^\circ`, as before, passing through points :g:`t` and :g:`l` and cutting the equinoctial at points :g:`m` and :g:`n`. And we shall draw line :g:`nem`, and likewise from the horizon center :g:`c`, placed as before,
straight lines :g:`ce` and :g:`ct`, and lastly also the perpendicular to line :g:`tl`, namely line :g:`cy`.

Therefore, as was stated above, arc :g:`am` is that difference by which Aries and Pisces each decrease in this clime from their rising in the right sphere, and the same by which each of the signs opposite them increases over its rising in the fixed sphere.
Now it is established that line :g:`et`, the semidiameter of the parallel circle designated at the beginning of Pisces, is :math:`73^p 39' 7''` out of those parts of which :math:`82^p 35' 3''` contain line :g:`ec`, namely the distance of the centers

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0457
   :page: p. 247

of the horizon and equinoctial.

Since, therefore, the excess of the square on :g:`tc` over the square on :g:`ec` is in :math:`3600^p` parts, if that number be divided by line :g:`et` and we pursue the rest in order as in the right sphere, we shall gather line :g:`ey` as before as :math:`12^p 22' 12''`.
Now out of those parts of which we count :math:`120^p` in line :g:`ec` opposite the right angle, line :g:`ey` will have :math:`18^p` parts and nearly one minute; the arc of whose chord is :math:`17^\circ 16'` out of the :math:`360^\circ` of the whole circle containing triangle :g:`ecy`.
Therefore, out of the :math:`360^\circ` that we count in four right angles, angle :g:`∠ecy` will have :math:`9^\circ 38'`; and since this is equal to angle :g:`∠tem`, :math:`9^\circ 38'` also measure arc :g:`am` out of the :math:`360^\circ` of the whole equinoctial circle.

Since, therefore, as was stated above, each of the four signs around the equinoctial points rises in the fixed sphere with :math:`27^\circ 50'`, when we subtract these :math:`9^\circ 38'` from that sum, there will remain the number of the rising of Aries and of Pisces in this clime,
namely :math:`19^\circ 12'`; whereas if we add those same :math:`9^\circ 38'` to the sum placed above, there will accrue the number of the rising of Virgo and of Libra, namely :math:`36^\circ 28'`.
