Section 12 · Notes
==================

Manuscript Numerals & *Almagest* II.8 Verification
--------------------------------------------------

1. **Scribal Error** (:math:`8^\circ 38'` vs. ``VIIII puncta XXXVIII``, :math:`9^\circ 38'`):
   On p. 247, lines 11–20, Hermann's Latin prints ``VIIII puncta XXXVIII`` (:math:`9^\circ 38'`) as half of :math:`17^\circ 16'`. Yet :math:`\frac{1}{2}(17^\circ 16') = 8^\circ 38'`, and Ptolemy's resulting ascensions—:math:`27^\circ 50' - 8^\circ 38' = 19^\circ 12'` and :math:`27^\circ 50' + 8^\circ 38' = 36^\circ 28'`—confirm that the original calculation used :math:`8^\circ 38'`.

2. **Line** :math:`ey` **Variant & Almagest II.8 Agreement**:
   Heiberg prints ``XII cum punctis XXII secundis XII`` on p. 247, line 5, noting in the critical apparatus that MS E reads ``23`` (:math:`12;23,12^p`, matching Section 8). In *Almagest* II.8 for Rhodes (:math:`14\frac{1}{2}^h`), Aries and Pisces rise in :math:`19^\circ 12'` and Virgo and Libra in :math:`36^\circ 28'`—exact agreement.
