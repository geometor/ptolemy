Section 13 · Rising Times of the Remaining Signs in the Inclined Sphere
=======================================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 13 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, pp. 247–249 <heiberg:1907>`)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0457
   :page: p. 247

By a similar example we may also measure the rising of the following signs, as when we cut off arc :g:`bt`
to the quantity of two signs—namely Pisces and Aquarius—until the rest are completed in the manner above.

Whence line :g:`et`, as the semidiameter of the parallel circle designated at the head of Aquarius, must increase until it contains :math:`86^p 29' 42''`.
When we divide the aforesaid difference :math:`3600` by this and complete the subsequent steps in order as before, we obtain as previously line :g:`ey` of :math:`22^p 25' 42''`. Therefore, out of the parts in which we number line :g:`ec` opposite the right angle as :math:`120^p`,
line :g:`ey` will contain :math:`32^p 36'`, whose chord belongs to an arc of :math:`31^\circ 32'` out of the :math:`360^\circ` of the whole circle circumscribing right triangle :g:`ecy`. Therefore, out of the degrees of which we number :math:`360^\circ` in four right angles, angle :g:`∠ecy` will have :math:`15^\circ 46'`.
Since this equals angle :g:`∠tem`, they measure arc :g:`am` as :math:`15^\circ 46'`, namely the excess of the rising of these two signs over their rising in the right sphere, which, as stated above, is measured by :math:`57^\circ 44'`.

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0459
   :page: p. 249

When we subtract :math:`15^\circ 46'` from this sum,
there remains the rising of Pisces together with Aquarius of :math:`41^\circ 58'`; whence, after subtracting the portion of Pisces, there remains the rising of Aquarius in :math:`22^\circ 46'`. But if we add those :math:`15^\circ 46'` to the aforesaid sum, the rising of Leo together with Virgo increases to :math:`73^\circ 30'`;
whence, after subtracting the portion of Virgo, there remains the rising of Leo of :math:`37^\circ 2'`. It is clear, moreover, that Taurus rises equally with Aquarius, and likewise Scorpio with Leo; for Gemini and Capricorn rise each in the remaining intervals of time of their respective quadrants,
just as Cancer and Sagittarius rise in the remaining intervals of their respective quadrants—which are :math:`29^\circ 17'` for Gemini and Capricorn, and :math:`35^\circ 15'` for Cancer and Sagittarius out of :math:`360^\circ` of the uniform circle, in the fourth clima of the island of Rhodes, which we take in the corporeal sphere as an example of the middle of the habitable climata, the rest to be constituted in the same manner by imitation of it.
