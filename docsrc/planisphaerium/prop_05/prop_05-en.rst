Section 5 · Parallel Circles at 30° from the Tropics
====================================================

*Planisphaerium*, Section 5 (:cite:`Heiberg 1907, Vol. II, p. 235 <heiberg:1907>`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/fig09_planisphaerium_stereographic_astrolabe.svg

.. folio:: heiberg_1907_opera_minora_vol2:0445
   :page: p. 235

Next we set each of the arcs :g:`gh` and :g:`gt` to :math:`20^\circ 30' 9''`, which is the distance between the equinoctial circle and the parallels thirty degrees along the zodiac below the tropic points. Then arc :g:`bt` will be :math:`110^\circ 30' 9''`, the chord of which is :math:`98^p 35' 59''`, while arc :g:`bh` will be :math:`69^\circ 29' 51''`,
the chord of which is :math:`68^p 23' 51''`.

Here, therefore, the ratio of :math:`98^p 35' 59''` to :math:`68^p 23' 51''` must be that of line :g:`ez` to line :g:`ed <BD_ast>` and of :g:`ed <BD_ast>` to line :g:`ek <EK>`; whence, out of the :math:`60^p` parts measuring line :g:`ed <BD_ast>`, :math:`86^p 29' 42''` must be numbered along line :g:`ez`, and :math:`41^p 39' 15''` along line :g:`ek <EK>`.
