Section 5 · Notes
=================

Solar Declination & Numerical Precision
---------------------------------------

1. **Almagest I.15 Declination**:
   The value :math:`\delta = 20^\circ 30' 9''` matches Ptolemy's *Table of Obliquity* in *Almagest* I.15 for :math:`60^\circ` from the vernal equinox (or :math:`30^\circ` from the solstitial points), marking the parallels through the beginnings of Gemini/Leo and Sagittarius/Aquarius.

2. **Exact Arithmetic & Manuscript Reading**:
   Exact division gives :math:`60 \times 98;35,59 / 68;23,51 \approx 86;29,39^p` and :math:`60 \times 68;23,51 / 98;35,59 \approx 41;37,17^p`. Heiberg's Latin text preserves :math:`86^p 29' 42''` and :math:`41^p 39' 15''` without apparatus variants, reflecting a scribal :math:`37 \leftrightarrow 39` shift in the northern radius.
