Section 11 · Summary
====================

Day-Length Verification for Rhodes (:math:`\varphi = 36^\circ`)
---------------------------------------------------------------

Using right triangle :math:`ect` (:math:`\angle etc = 90^\circ`), formed by the equinoctial–horizon center offset :math:`ec = 82;35,3^p` (Section 7) and the equinoctial–zodiac center offset :math:`et = 26;31,58^p` (Section 4):

- Rescaled to :math:`ec = 120^p`: :math:`et = 38;33^p = \operatorname{crd}(37^\circ 30')`.
- Ascensional difference at the solstices: :math:`ak = \angle aek = \angle ect = 18^\circ 45'`.
- Quadrant risings: :math:`71^\circ 15'` (vernal equinox side) and :math:`108^\circ 45'` (autumnal equinox side).
- Day-length deviation from :math:`12^h`: :math:`\pm 37^\circ 30' = \pm 2\frac{1}{2}` equinoctial hours (longest day :math:`14^h 30^m`, shortest :math:`9^h 30^m`).
