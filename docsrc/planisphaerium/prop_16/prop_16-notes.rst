Section 16 · Notes
==================

Stereographic Preservation of Small Circles
-------------------------------------------

1. **Euclidean Circle Theorems Used**:
   Ptolemy's proof establishes circle preservation for an inclined small circle without invoking Apollonius's *Conics* (subcontrary sections of an oblique cone). Instead, he applies Thales' theorem (:just:`Eucl. III.31 <III.31>`), the inscribed-angle theorem (:just:`Eucl. III.21–22 <III.21>`), and the intersecting-chord power theorem (:just:`Eucl. III.35 <III.35>`).

2. **Diagram & Apparatus Notes**:
   The figure on Heiberg p. 253 depicts points :g:`d, f, a, m, e, c, n, g, t, k, q, h, l, z, b, y, r`, where the intersection of :g:`bz` and :g:`kl` is labeled :g:`q`. In the apparatus on p. 253, *concurrant ad punctum r* carries minor manuscript variants for *procedent* and *quod postremo*.
