Section 16 · Summary
====================

Projecting a Small Circle Parallel to the Zodiac
------------------------------------------------

1. **Construction of the Projected Diameter** :math:`mn`:
   In meridian circle :math:`abgd` (south pole :math:`d`, equator :math:`ag`), the parallel to the zodiac has diameter :math:`zht`. Drawing :math:`kl \parallel ag` through :math:`h` and projecting rays :math:`dmz`, :math:`dcl`, and :math:`dnt` onto the equatorial diameter yields the projected diameter :math:`mn`.

2. **Proof of Circularity via Concyclic Quads**:
   Extending :math:`kl` and :math:`dt` to meet at :math:`r`, right angles at :math:`z` and :math:`h` make :math:`b, h, z, q` and :math:`b, d, r, q` concyclic, giving :math:`rh \cdot hq = bh \cdot hd = hl^2`. Since :math:`rq \parallel nm`, scaling from :math:`d` yields :math:`em \cdot en = ec^2 = ey \cdot ef`, proving :math:`n, y, m, f` are concyclic.
