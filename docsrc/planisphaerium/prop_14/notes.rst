Section 14 · Notes
==================

The *Aranea* & Ecliptic Star Mapping
------------------------------------

1. **Terminology: *Horoscopium* and *Aranea***:
   Hermann's phrase *in horoscopio instrumento aranea* renders Ptolemy's Greek terms for the planispheric astrolabe (*horoskopeion*) and its openwork rotating star-map plate (*arachnē*, "spider", later known in Latin as the *rete* or Arabic *al-`ankabūt*).

2. **Diagram & Apparatus Notes**:
   The figure on Heiberg p. 250 depicts the concentric circles :g:`abgd` and :g:`clm` around :g:`e` with auxiliary points :g:`z, h, t, k, n`. On p. 251 line 5, the subject :g:`et` before *extitit autem et equalis et equidistans gh* is implicitly understood from the preceding equality :g:`et = md`.
