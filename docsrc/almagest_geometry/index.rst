Table of Contents
-----------------

Composed at Alexandria around 150 CE by :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>` and dedicated to :person:`Syrus <syrus>`, the complete sixteen chapters of Book I of the :term:`Almagest <syntaxis-mathematike>` (*Μαθηματικὴ Σύνταξις*) constitute the supreme surviving synthesis of classical Greek cosmology, chord trigonometry (with the complete 360-row Table of Chords), and spherical Menelaus geometry (culminating in the Table of Obliquity and right-sphere ascensions). Each chapter provides synchronized reading tracks (**English**, **Greek**, **Summary**, and **Notes**).
