Unit I · Executive Summary
==========================

Deductive Structure of *Almagest* I.1–I.9 & I.10a
-------------------------------------------------

1. **Philosophical Grounding (*Almagest* I.1)**: Following :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` (*Metaphysics* E.1), :person:`Ptolemy <ptolemy>` locates mathematics as the unique theoretical science capable of unshakable demonstration (`βεβαίαν καὶ ἀμετάπειστον τὴν εἴδησιν`), mediating between invisible theology and mutable sublunary physics through arithmetic and geometry.
2. **Spherical Cosmology (*Almagest* I.2–I.9)**: Establishes the geometric frame of spherical astronomy—a central point-like Earth $O$ inside the rotating celestial sphere, organized around two great circles inclined at obliquity $\varepsilon$: the **celestial equator** and the **ecliptic**.
3. **The Sexagesimal Chord Circle (*Almagest* I.10a)**: Sets circumference $= 360^\circ$ and diameter $D = 120^p$ ($R = 60^p$), replacing Egyptian/Greek unit fractions (`μοναριασμοί`) with uniform base-60 positional fractions ($p; a, b = p + \frac{a}{60} + \frac{b}{3600}$).
4. **Single-Circle Golden Pentagon & Decagon Construction**: On semicircle $AB\Gamma$ with $\Delta B \perp A\Gamma$ and $E$ bisecting $\Delta\Gamma$, swinging $EB = 30\sqrt{5}$ onto $\Delta A$ at $Z$ yields in a single right triangle $\triangle B\Delta Z$:

   $$\Delta Z = s_{10} = \frac{R}{\phi} = 30(\sqrt{5} - 1), \qquad BZ = s_5 = \sqrt{R^2 + s_{10}^2} = 30\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}}.$$
