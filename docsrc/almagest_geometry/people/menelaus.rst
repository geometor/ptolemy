Menelaus
========

**Menelaus of Alexandria (Μενέλαος ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς, c. 70 – 140 CE)**
   Author of the *Sphaerica*; formulated the intrinsic geometry of spherical triangles and the six-quantity spherical transversal theorem adapted in *Almagest* I.13.
