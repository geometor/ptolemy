Heiberg
=======

**Johan Ludvig Heiberg (1854 – 1928)**
   Danish classical philologist who edited the definitive critical Teubner texts of Ptolemy's *Syntaxis Mathematica* (1898–1903) and *Opera Astronomica Minora* (1907), alongside Euclid and Archimedes.
