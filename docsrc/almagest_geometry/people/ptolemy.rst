Ptolemy
=======

**Claudius Ptolemy (Κλαύδιος Πτολεμαῖος, c. 100 – c. 170 CE)**
   Alexandrian mathematician, astronomer, geometer, and cartographer; author of the *Syntaxis Mathematica* (*Almagest*), *Planisphaerium*, *Geography*, *Harmonics*, and *Optics*.
