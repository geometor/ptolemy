Apollonius
==========

**Apollonius of Perga (Ἀπολλώνιος ὁ Περγαῖος, c. 240 – c. 190 BCE)**
   Author of the *Conics*; proved in *Conics* I.5 that the subcontrary section of an oblique circular cone is a true circle, underpinning the conformal circle-preservation of stereographic projection.
