Chapter 9 · On Particular Demonstrations
========================================

*Almagest* Book I, Chapter 9 (:cite:`Heiberg 1898, Vol. I.1, pp. 30–31 <heiberg:1898>`)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/fig01_celestial_sphere_preliminaries.svg

.. folio:: heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0040
   :page: p. 30

Such, then, in broad outline, may be the summary exposition of those matters that must be laid down in advance as preliminary hypotheses. As we are about to begin the particular demonstrations—of which we consider the first to be that by which the arc

.. folio:: heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0041
   :page: p. 31

between the aforementioned poles along the great circle described through them is determined in magnitude—we see that it is necessary first to set forth the treatment of the magnitude of straight lines in a circle, since we are going to demonstrate every single thing geometrically.
