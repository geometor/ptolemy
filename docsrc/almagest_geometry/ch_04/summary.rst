Chapter 4 · Summary
===================

East–West and North–South Proofs of Terrestrial Sphericity
----------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 4 (*Heiberg I, pp. 14–16*) demonstrates that the earth taken as a whole is sensibly spherical across both longitudinal and latitudinal axes:

* **East–West Curvature**: Lunar eclipses occur at proportionally later local hours for easterly observers, refuting concave, plane, and polygonal models.
* **North–South Curvature**: Traveling northward reveals northern stars and hides southern ones, refuting an east–west cylinder.
* **Sea Horizon**: Approaching mountains rise gradually out of the sea from every azimuth.
