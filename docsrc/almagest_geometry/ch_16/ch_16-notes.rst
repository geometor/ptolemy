Chapter 16 · Notes
==================

Editorial & Mathematical Notes
------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed directly from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 82–85 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0092``–``0095``), closing Book I (*Κλαυδίου Πτολεμαίου μαθηματικῶν αʹ*).
* **Bridge to the *Planisphaerium***: Ptolemy's *sphaera recta* rising times (:math:`27^\circ 50'` for the first sign and :math:`57^\circ 44'` for two signs) reappear verbatim in Sections 8–9 of the *Planisphaerium* (*Heiberg II, pp. 236–241*), where Ptolemy recomputes the exact same numbers stereographically in the equatorial plane to verify that his astrolabe projection reproduces the spherical trigonometry of Book I of the *Almagest*.
* **Sexagesimal Check**: On p. 82, line 24, :math:`2 ZB = 180^\circ - 47^\circ 42' 40'' = 132^\circ 17' 20''`, printed in Heiberg as ``ρλβ ιζ κ`` (with the `17'` corresponding to `17' 20''` rounding in the manuscripts).
