Editorial & Philosophical Notes
-------------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed directly from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 4–8 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0014``–``0018``).
* **Apparatus Variants**: At p. 4 l. 5, MSS C, D, and the lower margin of B insert Ptolemy's famous astronomical epigram (*Anthologia Palatina* 9.577) after the table of contents. At p. 5 l. 8, Heiberg references Aristotle's *Metaphysics* E.1 and Franz Boll's *Studien über Claudius Ptolemäus*.
