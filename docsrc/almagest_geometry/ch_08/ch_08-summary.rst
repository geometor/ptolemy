Chapter 8 · Summary
===================

The Two Primary Celestial Motions
---------------------------------

Chapter 8 (*Heiberg I, pp. 26–30*) distinguishes the two fundamental motions governing the heavens and defines the principal reference circles of spherical astronomy:

* **First Motion (Diurnal)**: Uniform east-to-west rotation of the entire heavens about the equatorial poles, parallel to the celestial equator and bounded by the meridian.
* **Second Motion (Planetary)**: Slower, opposite (west-to-east) revolution of the sun, moon, and planets about the poles of the oblique ecliptic, defining the vernal/autumnal equinoxes and summer/winter solstices.
