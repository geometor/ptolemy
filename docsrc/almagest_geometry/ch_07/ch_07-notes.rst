Chapter 7 · Notes
=================

Editorial & Physical Notes
--------------------------

* **Folio Provenance**: Transcribed from :person:`J. L. Heiberg <heiberg>` (*Syntaxis Mathematica*, Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898), pp. 21–26 (folios ``heiberg_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0031``–``0036``).
* **Kinematics & Apparatus Variants**: Ptolemy concedes on p. 24 that diurnal terrestrial rotation could save stellar appearances on the simpler view, appealing instead to atmospheric and projectile physics. At p. 24 l. 17, MS A reads ``τῶν ἐν ἀέρι`` against ``τὸν ἀέρα`` in MSS B, C, and D.
