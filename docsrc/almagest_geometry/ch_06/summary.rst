Chapter 6 · Summary
===================

The Earth as a Point Relative to the Heavens
--------------------------------------------

Chapter 6 (*Heiberg I, pp. 20–21*) establishes that the earth has the ratio of a mathematical point relative to the sphere of the fixed stars, supported by three empirical criteria:

* **Constancy of Star Sizes and Intervals**: Stellar magnitudes and angular separations appear identical from every terrestrial latitude.
* **Instrument Equivalence**: Gnomons and armillary spheres at the surface behave as though placed at the earth's true center.
* **Horizon Bisection**: Every local tangent plane bisects the celestial sphere into equal hemispheres.
