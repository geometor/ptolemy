geometor.ptolemy.stereographic¶

Exact symbolic 3D-to-2D Stereographic Projection & Astrolabe Geometry (Planisphaerium).

Implements Ptolemy’s Planisphaerium (ed. J. L. Heiberg, Opera Astronomica Minora, Vol. II, Leipzig: Teubner, 1907, §§1-4, pp. 227-234):

  1. Exact Rational Stereographic Bijection (stereographic_forward(), stereographic_inverse()): Projects $P = (X, Y, Z) \in S^2 \setminus \{(0, 0, -1)\}$ from the South Pole $S = (0, 0, -1)$ onto the equatorial plane $Z = 0$:

    $$(x, y) = \left(\frac{X}{1 + Z},\, \frac{Y}{1 + Z}\right), \qquad (X, Y, Z) = \left(\frac{2x}{1 + x^2 + y^2},\, \frac{2y}{1 + x^2 + y^2},\, \frac{1 - x^2 - y^2}{1 + x^2 + y^2}\right)$$

  2. Universal Circle-Preservation & Conformal Metric Theorems (project_sphere_circle_plane(), prove_circle_preservation_symbolically(), prove_conformal_metric_symbolically(), prove_parallel_inversion_symbolically()): Proves in exact SymPy algebra over $\mathbb{Q}[a, b, c, d, x, y]$ that every plane $aX + bY + cZ + d = 0$ cutting $S^2$ without passing through $S$ ($d \neq c$) projects to an exact Euclidean circle in the equatorial plane, that meridians ($d = c$) project to straight lines through the North Pole, that opposite parallels at \(\pm\delta\) obey circular inversion \(r_+ \cdot r_- = R_{\mathrm{eq}}^2\) (Planisphaerium §4, p. 233: ez : ed = ed : ek), and that the pulled-back metric is conformally flat \(dX^2 + dY^2 + dZ^2 = \frac{4}{(1 + x^2 + y^2)^2}(dx^2 + dy^2)\).

  3. Exact ``geometor.model`` Astrolabe Tropics, Ecliptic & Almucantar Construction (construct_astrolabe_planisphere(), construct_almucantar_circle()): Constructs Ptolemy’s Planisphaerium §1 diagram (Heiberg 1907, p. 227: equator $ABGD$, North Pole $E$, Tropic of Cancer $TL$, Tropic of Capricorn $KM$, and oblique ecliptic circle $MBTD$ centered at $R$) in geometor.model with auto_intersect=False and zero sp.Float coordinates.

geometor.ptolemy.stereographic.project_sphere_circle_plane(a: sympy.Expr, b: sympy.Expr, c: sympy.Expr, d: sympy.Expr) → ProjectedCircleData¶

Project the spherical circle cut on $S^2$ by plane $aX + bY + cZ + d = 0$.

Substituting $(X, Y, Z) = \left(\frac{2x}{1+r^2}, \frac{2y}{1+r^2}, \frac{1-r^2}{1+r^2}\right)$ with $r^2 = x^2 + y^2$ yields:

$$(d - c)(x^2 + y^2) + 2ax + 2by + (d + c) = 0$$

  • When $d = c$ (plane passes through South Pole $S = (0, 0, -1)$), the locus is the exact straight line $ax + by + c = 0$.

  • When $d \neq c$, completing the square yields an exact Euclidean circle with center $(-\frac{a}{d-c}, -\frac{b}{d-c})$ and squared radius $\frac{a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - d^2}{(d-c)^2}$.

geometor.ptolemy.stereographic.prove_conformal_metric_symbolically() → sympy.Expr¶

Prove conformality of stereographic projection by computing the Jacobian metric tensor $J^T J$.

Verifies that for $\Phi(x, y) = (X(x, y), Y(x, y), Z(x, y))$,

$$J^T J = \frac{4}{(1 + x^2 + y^2)^2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

so angles between intersecting curves on the sphere are preserved identically in the plane.

geometor.ptolemy.stereographic.prove_parallel_inversion_symbolically() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Prove Planisphaerium §4 (Heiberg 1907, p. 233): opposite parallels satisfy \(r_+ \cdot r_- = R^2\).

Parameterizing \(\sin\delta = \frac{2t}{1+t^2}, \cos\delta = \frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\) (\(0 < t < 1\)), the parallel at \(+\delta\) (\(Z = \sin\delta\)) has projected radius \(r_+ = R \frac{1 - t}{1 + t}\) and the southern parallel at \(-\delta\) (\(Z = -\sin\delta\)) has radius \(r_- = R \frac{1 + t}{1 - t}\), giving \(r_+ \cdot r_- = R^2\) (ez : ed = ed : ek).

geometor.ptolemy.stereographic.construct_astrolabe_planisphere(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, *, equator_radius: int | sympy.Rational = 60, half_obliquity_tan: sympy.Rational = sp.Rational(1, 5)) → AstrolabePlanisphereResult¶

Construct Ptolemy’s Planisphaerium §§1 & 4 astrolabe plate in geometor.model.

Follows Heiberg 1907, pp. 227-229 & 233 letter labels ($ato A, bto B, gto G, dto D, eto E, tto T, lto L, kto K, mto M, rto R$):

  • Equator $ABGD$ centered at North Pole $E = (0, 0)$ with orthogonal diameters $AG$ (vertical) and $BD$ (horizontal).

  • With rational half-obliquity parameter $u = \tan(\varepsilon / 2)$ (default $u = \frac{1}{5}$, so $\sin\varepsilon = \frac{5}{13} \implies \varepsilon \approx 22^\circ 37'$, 100% in $\mathbb{Q}$!), the Tropic of Cancer circle $TL$ has radius $r_{\mathrm{Can}} = R \frac{1 - u}{1 + u}$ ($= 40$ when $R = 60, u = \frac{1}{5}$) and the Tropic of Capricorn circle $KM$ has radius $r_{\mathrm{Cap}} = R \frac{1 + u}{1 - u}$ ($= 90$ when $R = 60, u = \frac{1}{5}$), satisfying $40 \times 90 = 3600 = 60^2$ (Planisphaerium §4)!

  • Bisecting segment $TM$ ($T = (0, -r_{\mathrm{Can}})$, $M = (0, r_{\mathrm{Cap}})$) at $R = (0, \frac{r_{\mathrm{Cap}} - r_{\mathrm{Can}}}{2})$ ($= (0, 25)$), the circle on diameter $TM$ centered at $R$ has radius $\frac{r_{\mathrm{Cap}} + r_{\mathrm{Can}}}{2} = 65$ and passes exactly through opposite equatorial points $B = (-60, 0)$ and $D = (60, 0)$ since $25^2 + 60^2 = 65^2$ (Planisphaerium §1: $\angle MDT = 90^\circ$)!

geometor.ptolemy.stereographic.construct_almucantar_circle(sin_latitude: sympy.Rational, cos_latitude: sympy.Rational, sin_altitude: sympy.Rational, *, equator_radius: int | sympy.Rational = 60) → ProjectedCircleData¶

Compute the exact projected astrolabe almucantar (constant-altitude circle) over $\mathbb{Q}$.

For observer latitude $\varphi$ (zenith $Z_0 = (0, \cos\varphi, \sin\varphi)$ on $S^2$) and altitude $h$ above the horizon ($h = 0$ gives Ptolemy’s Planisphaerium §§2-3 horizon!), the almucantar plane on the unit sphere is:

$$\cos\varphi \cdot Y + \sin\varphi \cdot Z - \sin h = 0$$

When $h = 0$ (horizon), $d = 0$, and the projected horizon circle passes through the two diametrically opposite equatorial East/West points $(\pm R, 0)$.
