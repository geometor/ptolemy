geometor.ptolemy.menelaus_sphaerica¶

Exact symbolic Planar & Spherical Menelaus Lemmas (Almagest Book I, Chs. 13-14).

Implements Heiberg 1898, Vol. I.1, pp. 68-78:

  1. Planar Menelaus Lemma (construct_planar_menelaus(), Almagest I.13, pp. 68-70): Constructs Ptolemy’s sector-figure ($AB, AC$ cut by $BE, CD$ meeting at $Z$) and draws auxiliary line $EH \parallel CD$ meeting $AB$ at $H$ (Eucl. I.31, VI.4), proving both kata synthesin ($\frac{CA}{AE} = \frac{CD}{DZ} \cdot \frac{ZB}{BE}$) and kata diairesin ($\frac{CE}{EA} = \frac{CZ}{ZD} \cdot \frac{DB}{BA}$).

  2. Spherical Menelaus Chord Theorem & Solar Declination (verify_spherical_menelaus(), compute_solar_declination(), Almagest I.13-I.14, pp. 74-78): Verifies Ptolemy’s 3D central projection from the sphere center $H$ onto the plane of $\triangle ACD$ (producing collinear meet points $\Theta, K, \Lambda$ on two intersecting planes, Heiberg I, p. 75) and computes Ptolemy’s exact chord ratio $\operatorname{crd}(2\delta) = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\varepsilon)\operatorname{crd}(2\lambda)}{120}$.

geometor.ptolemy.menelaus_sphaerica.construct_planar_menelaus(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → PlanarMenelausResult¶

Construct Ptolemy’s planar Menelaus sector configuration (Almagest I.13, Heiberg I, pp. 68-70).

Follows Heiberg’s Greek letters ($mathrm{A}to A, mathrm{B}to B, mathrm{Gamma}to C, mathrm{Delta}to D, mathrm{E}to E, mathrm{Z}to Z, mathrm{H}to H$): Across two straight lines $AB$ and $AC$, two transversals $BE$ and $CD$ meet at $Z$, and $EH \parallel CD$ meets $AB$ at $H$.

geometor.ptolemy.menelaus_sphaerica.verify_spherical_menelaus() → SphericalMenelausResult¶

Verify Ptolemy’s 3D central-projection proof of Spherical Menelaus (Almagest I.13, pp. 74-76).

Constructs an exact rational spherical Menelaus configuration $(A, B, C, D, E, Z)$ on $S^2$ centered at $H = (0, 0, 0)$, computes the three planar intersections $\Theta = AD \cap HB$, $K = CD \cap HZ$, and $\Lambda = AC \cap HE$ (Heiberg I, p. 75), verifies that $\Theta, K, \Lambda$ are strictly collinear (lying in the intersection of plane $ACD$ with great-circle plane $HBZE$), and verifies Ptolemy’s exact spherical chord theorem:

$$\frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{CE})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{EA})} = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{CZ})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{ZD})} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{DB})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{BA})}$$

geometor.ptolemy.menelaus_sphaerica.compute_solar_declination(longitude_deg: int) → SolarDeclinationEntry¶

Compute Ptolemy’s Almagest I.14–I.15 solar declination for $\lambda \in \{30^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ\}$.

Reproduces Heiberg 1898, Vol. I.1, pp. 77-78 verbatim: With obliquity $2\varepsilon = \frac{11}{83} \cdot 360^\circ = 47^\circ 42' 40''$ and Ptolemy’s chord $\operatorname{crd}(2\varepsilon) = 48;31,55$, Menelaus’s theorem gives:

$$\operatorname{crd}(2\delta) = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\varepsilon) \cdot \operatorname{crd}(2\lambda)}{120}$$

yielding $\delta(30^\circ) \approx 11^\circ 40'$ (Heiberg p. 78, l. 12: $\overline{\iota\alpha}\ \overline{\mu}$) and $\delta(60^\circ) \approx 20^\circ 30' 9''$ (Heiberg p. 78, l. 24: $\overline{\kappa}\ \overline{\lambda}\ \overline{\theta}$).
