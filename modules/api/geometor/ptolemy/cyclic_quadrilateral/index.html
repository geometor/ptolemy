geometor.ptolemy.cyclic_quadrilateral¶

Exact symbolic construction & proof of Ptolemy’s Theorem on Cyclic Quadrilaterals.

Implements Almagest Book I, Ch. 10b (Heiberg 1898, Vol. I.1, pp. 36-37):

\[AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot DA\]

Includes:

  1. Exact Euclidean Construction & Proof (construct_ptolemy_quadrilateral()): Constructs canonical rational cyclic quadrilaterals in geometor.model with auto_intersect=False and zero sp.Float coordinates—including the classical Pythagorean-chord cyclic quadrilateral on diameter $65$ with integer sides $(AB, BC, CD, DA) = (25, 39, 60, 52)$ and integer diagonals $(AC, BD) = (56, 63)$ ($56 \times 63 = 3528 = 25 \times 60 + 39 \times 52 = 1500 + 2028$), as well as Brahmagupta’s $(13, 14, 15)$-derived cyclic quadrilateral—together with Ptolemy’s auxiliary point $E \in AC$ satisfying $\angle ABE = \angle DBC$.

  2. Universal Algebraic Proof (prove_ptolemy_theorem_universally()): Proves Ptolemy’s identity universally over $\mathbb{Q}(t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4)$ on the rational parameterization of the circle via Cayley-Menger / Euler four-point identity.

geometor.ptolemy.cyclic_quadrilateral.construct_ptolemy_quadrilateral(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, *, preset: str = 'pythagorean_65') → CyclicQuadrilateralResult¶

Construct a rational cyclic quadrilateral and verify Ptolemy’s Theorem (Almagest I.10, pp. 36-37).

Supported preset configurations (100% exact rational coordinates):

  • "pythagorean_65" (default): Cyclic quadrilateral on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = (65/2)^2$ with exact rational vertices $A = (-65/2, 0)$, $B = (-595/26, 300/13)$, $C = (2047/130, 1848/65)$, and $D = (91/10, -156/5)$, yielding exact integer sides $(AB, BC, CD, DA) = (25, 39, 60, 52)$ and exact integer diagonals $(AC, BD) = (56, 63)$, so $56 \times 63 = 3528 = 25 \times 60 + 39 \times 52$.

  • "brahmagupta": Rational orthodiagonal cyclic quadrilateral on $x^2 + y^2 = (65/2)^2$ with integer sides $(25, 39, 60, 52)$ in swapped chord order so $AC \perp BD$.

Parameters:

  • model – Optional Model (created with auto_intersect=False if None).

  • preset – "pythagorean_65" or "brahmagupta".

Returns:

Verified CyclicQuadrilateralResult.

geometor.ptolemy.cyclic_quadrilateral.prove_ptolemy_theorem_universally() → sympy.Expr¶

Prove Ptolemy’s Theorem universally over $\mathbb{Z}[t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4]$ on the rational circle.

On the unit circle parameterized by $P(t) = \left(\frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2}, \frac{2t}{1 + t^2}\right)$, the Euclidean chord distance between $P(t_i)$ and $P(t_j)$ for ordered parameters $t_1 < t_2 < t_3 < t_4$ is:

$$d(t_i, t_j) = \frac{2(t_j - t_i)}{\sqrt{(1 + t_i^2)(1 + t_j^2)}}$$

Clearing the common positive denominator $\frac{1}{4}(1+t_1^2)(1+t_2^2)(1+t_3^2)(1+t_4^2)$ reduces $AC \cdot BD - (AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot DA) = 0$ to Euler’s universal four-point polynomial identity, returning sp.Integer(0).
